# Course Description

# **Welcome to JobCamp!**

Welcome to **JobCamp - Metana’s Career Acceleration Program!** Over the next few weeks, you’ll go through an intensive, outcome-driven journey designed to help you secure a high-paying role in tech. This program focuses entirely on employability: sharpening your personal brand, strengthening your portfolio, preparing you for interviews, and guiding you until you land the role you’re aiming for.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8SclGmkt61g>

# **Program Overview**

Throughout JobCamp, you will:

- Build a **high-impact resume and LinkedIn profile** that stands out to global recruiters.
- Create or refine your **GitHub and technical portfolio**, showcasing real, hire-ready projects.
- Practice **behavioral and technical interview strategies** with guidance from industry mentors.
- Improve your **communication skills, storytelling, and personal positioning** to match employer expectations.
- Learn how to **apply strategically**, track your applications, and optimize your job-search efficiency.
- Get weekly **1:1 career coaching**, accountability, and personalized feedback.
- Understand **how to negotiate offers**, handle rejections, and maintain momentum.

# **Learning Outcomes**

By the end of JobCamp, you will:

- Have a **professional, recruiter-optimized resume and LinkedIn profile**.
- Present a **well-structured portfolio** that reflects your technical capability.
- Demonstrate strong confidence in **behavioral and technical interview environments**.
- Know how to **navigate job portals**, maximize response rates, and strategically target companies.
- Understand how to **negotiate salary**, handle multiple offers, and present yourself as a strong candidate.
- Have a clear system to **track applications, follow up with recruiters**, and continuously improve your chances.
