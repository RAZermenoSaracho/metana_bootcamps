# Time to submit new quality applications

You’re well on your way to mastering effective outreach strategies. It’s time to continue submitting high-quality job applications to follow up on!

Job searching can be quite demanding on your time. So here’s a helpful tip for you: **create job alerts**. Most job boards offer a feature to send you new job postings that align with your specific criteria directly to your email. This can be a great time-saver. The tutorial below will help you set up Google and LinkedIn job alerts.

## Proceeding Forward

Hopefully, you’ve found some intriguing roles while setting up your job alerts on LinkedIn, Google, or other platforms. Now, utilize the your [job tracker](https://app.careerflow.ai/?invite=metana) to select **TEN** job opportunities to apply for before the week ends. Ensure these opportunities meet the following criteria:

☑️ You match at least 50% of the job requirements

☑️ The job listing is less than two weeks old

☑️ You are applying through the company’s official website

☑️ Use a resume that highlights the skills and keywords required for the job

After applying for **TEN** additional opportunities, the most critical next step is to ensure a human eye sees your application.

☑️ Send a note to a talent or technical professional at the company

Use the ‘applied’ column in your [job tracker](https://app.careerflow.ai/?invite=metana) to identify the **TEN** opportunities that require a follow-up. Draw attention to your application by sending a simple LinkedIn note or email to a hiring or technical professional at the company you’ve applied to.

For instance…

**Reach out to a recruiter or talent acquisition professional saying:**

> Hi [THEIR NAME],
>
> While researching [company name], I noticed you are a [recruiter/talent title] there. If it’s convenient for you this week, could we discuss how my skills and expertise in [mention skills from job description] align with the [role title] role I applied for? I’ve attached my resume for your reference.

Alternatively,

**Message a technical professional with:**

> Hi [THEIR NAME],
>
> While exploring [company name], I noticed you are a [Software Engineer/Data Analyst/etc.] there. If you have some free time this week, could we discuss your experience at [company]? I recently applied for the [role title] and would greatly appreciate your insights.
