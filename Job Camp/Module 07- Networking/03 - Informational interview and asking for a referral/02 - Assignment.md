# Assignment

#### By following this action task list, you can effectively conduct informational interviews, build meaningful connections, and increase your chances of obtaining a referral for the job you’re interested in.

1. **Preparation**
   - Identify and list 5 potential contacts.
   - Draft and send a personalized request message to each contact.
   - Research the company’s recent news and the contact’s role.
2. **Questions** **you can ask from your contact**
   - “How did you get started at [Company Name]?”
   - “What does a typical day look like in your role?”
   - “What qualities are essential for someone in the [Role Title]?”
3. **Referral Request**
   - “How can I stand out in the application process for [Role Title]?

***Note: Always make sure that they are comfortable with introducing you with recommended person and if they are comfortable, then go a referral request***    
  
**Always remember to Follow-Up**

- Send a thank-you email and show your gratitude
- Connect on LinkedIn and send periodic updates.
