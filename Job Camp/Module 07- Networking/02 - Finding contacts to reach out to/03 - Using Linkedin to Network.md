# Using Linkedin to Network

### **Use LinkedIn to find contacts**

[Video: Job Seekers on LinkedIn Need to Know These 8 Things](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7DIHNK2DYw)

**Option 1:** Navigate to the company’s page on LinkedIn and go to the People tab. Search using keywords like “Engineering” or “Data Analyst” to find relevant contacts within the company.

**Option 2:** Enter this search term into Google: site:www.linkedin.com/in -inurl:dir “company name” “keyword: engineer, recruiter”. This search query can help you find LinkedIn profiles of individuals working at the specific company you are interested in, with keywords related to the role such as “engineer” or “recruiter”.

### **Use Clearbit’s email extension to find the email**

To find a contact’s email, you can use Clearbit’s email extension. This extension is available for Google Chrome and can be installed from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/clearbit-connect-supercha/pmnhcgfcafcnkbengdcanjablaabjplo?hl=en).

Once you have installed the Clearbit Connect extension, you can follow these steps:

1. Open the contact’s LinkedIn profile or visit their company’s website.
2. Click on the Clearbit Connect extension icon in your browser’s toolbar.
3. The extension will search for the contact’s email and provide you with the results if it finds any.
4. If an email is found, you can copy it or use the options provided by Clearbit Connect to directly send an email.

If you need visual guidance on how to use Clearbit Connect, you can watch a short 2-minute video tutorial on how to use it [here](https://www.loom.com/share/befff1dbf7c84cb2ac3fb78d745c3334).

Please note that finding someone’s email using Clearbit or any other similar tool might not always be successful, as it depends on the availability and accuracy of publicly available information.

These options can help you identify potential contacts who may be relevant to your job search or provide insights into the company. Good luck!
