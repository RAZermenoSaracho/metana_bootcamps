# Emailing Recruiters

‼️ Before you reach out to a recruiter, pause and make sure that you have exhausted your search for someone on the engineering team. Recruiters get a lot of outreach, so you’ll stand out from the crowd more with direct outreach to the hiring manager.

#### Attention-grabbing, “fun” templates. Ideal for recruiters at startups.

**Template 1**

code

```
Subject Line: Super excited about {company} OR I think I’m your next {role title}! OR Raising my hand for {role title}!

Hi there,

I hope this email finds you well amidst the flood of applicants you must be receiving since the {role title} dropped. I'll keep this brief for someone who, admittedly, has a lot to say about my love for {company name} ?.

Your job description resonated with me on multiple levels! I genuinely enjoy coding and thrive in fast-paced development environments. With deep experience in React, Node.js, and HTML/CSS frontends, I'm confident in my ability to write well-tested and efficient code. Additionally, I noticed that experience with data teams *and* collaborating with clients directly is valuable for this role. Luckily, I gained expertise in these areas during a recent project with {Labs stakeholder}, which I believe would add value to the team. I've already applied through the listing on your website but wanted to attach my resume for your reference as well.

I would love the opportunity to chat more about your team and what you're seeking in your next Software Engineer hire. Are you available for a quick phone call this week?

All the best,  
  
{name}  
{Contact}
```

**Template 2**

code

```
Subject Line: Re: {role title}

Hi [name],

First off, I have to thank you for offering me the Front End Engineer role at {company name}! I enthusiastically accept. Okay, okay, I know we’re not *quite* there yet.

But if we were, here are just a few ideas of what I would do once in the role:
- Leveraging my strong background in React and design fundamentals, I would jump in headfirst to develop clean, modern, and modular user interfaces. I noticed that experience building UI Libraries was a "nice to have" for this role, and luckily, it's something I'm proficient in.
- With my genuine love for bug-squashing, I would diligently document and fix bugs, easing the pressure on the engineering team. As the new person on the team, I believe it's important to contribute in any way possible.
- Drawing on my experience working with fully remote teams, I would quickly adapt to the team's communication and development practices to ensure efficient code delivery.

These are just some initial ideas. I would love to learn more about this role and the team's specific needs. Are you still conducting interviews for this position? If so, would you be available for a quick chat this week?

{name}  
{Contact}
```

#### Straightforward templates. Ideal for recruiters at more formal companies.

**Template 1**

code

```
Subject Line: Super excited about {company} OR I think I’m your next {role title}! OR Connect? OR Raising my hand for {role title}!

Hello [name],

I am incredibly excited about {company name} and the problems your Engineering team is working on. I feel a deep connection to the company's mission of [briefly mention the mission]. 

I'm [name], and I'm extremely interested in joining your team. I recently applied for the {position title} you posted on the company website and I'm thrilled about the opportunity. To give you some insight into my background, I have extensive experience in [skill from job description], [skill from job description], and [skill from job description]. I recently completed a project that aligns well with this role, and I've included a link to it here: [link to GitHub].

Are you available this week for a chat? I have open availability and would love to hear more about what you're looking for in your next {position title}.

Speak soon!
{name}  
{contact}
```

**Template 2**

code

```
Subject Line: Use this template for applications submitted via email.

Hi [name],

Good morning/afternoon! 

As a recent graduate from the {program name} at BloomTech, a full-time intensive and immersive project-based curriculum program, I recently applied for the [position title] on LinkedIn and I'm truly excited about the potential opportunity!

I believe I'm a great fit for the role, and here's why [pick the top 3 things the company needs from the job description and connect them to yourself]:

1. I am passionate about writing high-quality, fast, and clean code. I have deep experience with structured code, databases, object-oriented programming, and "specialty" languages.
2. I am relentlessly self-motivated and take proactive actions seriously when working on a team. I believe in ensuring tasks are completed and expectations are communicated, fostering a collaborative work environment.
3. I am skilled at translating technical decisions to non-technical audiences. I strive to avoid the common pitfall of technical professionals speaking over the heads of their customers.

I would love to connect and delve deeper into my background, as well as discuss what I can bring to the team. Are you available for a phone or video call on Thursday or Friday of this week?

Thank you for your time and consideration,

{name}  
{Contact}
```
