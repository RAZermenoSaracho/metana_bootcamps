### **Resume basics and ordering**

These are few important points that you need consider when building the best resume:

1. On the headline mention Job role that you want and tech stack that you have used thats aligned with the job role
2. Mention what are you learning now and what your next plans
3. Give meaningful title for you past work experience
4. 3 to 5 point what you did on the previous projects, what results and benefits you brought for the company
5. Put the most valuable section at high up, so that’s the first thing a recruiter or employer can see. This builds so much value for resume and makes the recruiter read your resume more in depth and refer to the evidences to see how good you are

### **What are the ATS systems**

[Video: A Guide to Applicant Tracking Systems | Resume Tips | Indeed Career Tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DksA_vF84JA)

Job applicants are often worry too much about the ATS system, best solution you can take is to make your resume clear for humans to read. Focus more on adding the relevant information in to your profile rather than focusing on the format too much. As long as you develop a decent resume , your safe from the ATS system
