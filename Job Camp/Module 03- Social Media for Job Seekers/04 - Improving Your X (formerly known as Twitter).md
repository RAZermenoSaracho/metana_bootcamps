# Improving Your X (formerly known as Twitter)

Let’s not solely rely on LinkedIn to build a personal brand and network. It’s important to spread your voice on other platforms as well

[Video: How I Built An Audience on Twitter/X (As a Beginner)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=31KpLIJXFgM)

**Define your target companies -** Make a list of companies in the web3 space that you’re interested in working for. Research their Twitter profiles and identify the recruiters or hiring managers that are active on the platform.

**Optimize your X profile -**Make sure your X profile is professional and up-to-date. Use a clear headshot as your profile picture, and make sure your bio includes keywords related to your skills and experience. Understand that you do not have to be overly professional. Do not use something like a Passport picture or your national ID picture.

**Share relevant content -**Share links to blog posts, open-source contributions, or other work that showcases your skills and interests. Make sure the content you share is relevant to the web3 space and highlights your expertise.

**Engage with recruiters and companies -**Follow the recruiters and hiring managers you identified in step one, and engage with their tweets. This could include retweeting or commenting on their posts, asking thoughtful questions, or sharing your own insights related to the industry.

**Use relevant hashtags -**Incorporate relevant hashtags into your tweets to make sure your content is discoverable by recruiters and potential employers. This could include hashtags related to the industry, job titles, or specific skills.

**Participate in X chats, communities, spaces -**Find relevant X chats, communities, spaces related to the web3 space, and participate by sharing your thoughts and engaging with others. This is a great way to demonstrate your expertise and connect with others in the industry.

**Connect with others in the industry -**Use Twitter to connect with others in the web3 space, such as developers, designers, or other professionals. This can help you build relationships and gain insights into the industry.

**Monitor job postings -**Keep an eye out for job postings from the companies you’re interested in. When you see a relevant job posting, reach out to the recruiter or hiring manager on X to express your interest and highlight your skills and experience. You can follow and turn post notifications on, an account like Cryptojoblists (which is a Web3 Job board), they tweet job opportunities whenever they are available.

Remember that using X to land a job in the web3 space is a long-term strategy. It’s important to be consistent in your engagement, share relevant content, and build relationships with others in the industry. By executing this strategic plan consistently, you can increase your visibility to recruiters and potentially land your dream job in the web3 space.
