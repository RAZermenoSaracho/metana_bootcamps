When it comes to Personal Brand, you can divide it into two sections known as “What” and “ Why “

### What is Personal Branding?

Personal branding is the process of establishing and promoting a unique personal brand that identifies and communicates your skills, experience, values, and personality. It involves strategically managing how you present yourself to others, both online and offline.

### And now “ Why “ Personal Brand

Building a personal brand has a lot of benefits, but here we are going to focus on.

- Building an audience of your own
- Making you known as One of the best devs in web3 – brand
- An authoritative Figure
- The inbound influx of job offers
- Landing a Job
