### Why do you need to write Blogs?

Creating content on social media is good but coupling it with blogging makes it better. Here are some reasons why we want you to blog

- Blogging showcases that you have in-depth knowledge
- Adds to your authority from LinkedIn
- Builds another audience
- Improves your Personel Brand

### Fundamentals of Writing

When it comes to articles we have some best practices that we recommend you follow:

- Formatting is important with proper tags (H1,H2 etc)
- Your content should help a person go from beginner to intermediate in a particular topic
- Language readability grade should be less than 8
- The article should be easy to read

### What are the Platforms?

These are some of the platforms that you can publish your blog article :

- Your website blog
- Linkedin Articles
- Medium
- Hashnode
- Metana Blogs
