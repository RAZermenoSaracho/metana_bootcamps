# Posting Content

As Job seeker in the modern day it is important that you focus on building content related to your specific industry, skills and the projects you do.

### **What to Create ?**

When it comes to creating valuable content, there are a few important things that you need to consider:

1. Understanding your target audience
2. Creating content that highlights your skills and experience

Your audience consists of fellow developers like you, recruiters, and employers. It is important that you study your audience by doing the following:

- Go through your current connections and check what they engage with
- Find out what people are talking about related to your niche
- Go through groups related to your niche

The next important action is to create content that caters to what your target audience is looking for and highlights your skills.

For example, if your target audience is interested in Smart Contract Development, you can create content about the projects you did in that field. This will help you build better connections among fellow developers on LinkedIn and, at the same time, get noticed by recruiters and potential employers.

### **How to create a post ?**

If your a total beginner for content creation here’s small outline that you can follow to get an idea for the first post :

Let’s imagine you are working on **some** code in building smart contracts, you can take a screenshot of the code and make sure you explain these 3 points below in your post

1. Introduction to the code and what it does
2. The key learning areas that of this project
3. Challenges that you encountered and solutions that you took

Following this method can help you create a post easily, the next is up to you. I want you to do your own research and be creative when it comes to content creation. Study how other students of Metana creates contents and instead of copying their style i want you to come up with an improvement

### **Why you need to Document your journey**

Quick question, there are two guys with two Rubik’s cubes. One guy says he completed the Rubik’s cube, and another **guy** completed the Rubik’s cube in front of others. Whom do you trust more? Yeah, exactly, the guy who completed the Rubik’s cube in front of you.

This is exactly why smart developers document their journey, because they always have evidence to prove your determination, proactiveness and skill.

### **Why are you not posting ?**

Is the reason for not posting because your code or project is incomplete? As we discussed in the “documenting your journey” section, you don’t have to create perfect code or the perfect project to create content. Just share your current progress, and in that post, mention what you plan to do next.

### **Why One post is not enough** ?

Many developers wait until a project is completed to create content; this is the wrong approach. On platforms like LinkedIn, where numerous posts compete for the attention of your target audience, do you really think posting just once will make you stand out? You might want to reconsider this strategy.

When documenting your journey, it’s crucial to create multiple posts to demonstrate your enthusiasm and proactivity to your target audience. By creating several posts, you can discuss different scenarios or projects where you applied your technical knowledge. This is particularly beneficial when recruiters are screening your profile. Why? When you consistently produce content about the technology you’ve used in various posts, it allows recruiters to see how you’ve implemented the tech stack they require, making you an ideal candidate for consideration.
