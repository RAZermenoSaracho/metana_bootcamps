# Assignment

Complete these below mentioned action task before moving into other lessons

### **Setting up the Banner Image**

- Make it relevant and current
- About your tech stack or niche
- Services provided

### **Profile Picture**

- Professional
- Make sure your face takes up at least 60% of the frame
- Be the only person in the picture
- Get someone else to take the picture for you
- Avoid distracting backgrounds
- Wear what you’d wear to work
- Use filters wisely

### **Headline**

- Show your skills and expertise (what do you do?)
- Include current or past job titles if those are relevant to the jobs you’re pursuing now
- Communicate the value you bring to a new employer (why should they care?)
- Include at least one keyword/phrase for the type of job you want, whether a job title or other keyword
- Demonstrate something unique to make your LinkedIn profile stand out – Maybe it’s a specific accomplishment. Or an award. Maybe it’s a passion or a hobby.  
    
  **These are some Templates you can use**
- Role | Specific Achievement
- Role | Years of Experience in Industry | Fun Fact to Stand Out
- Role | Industry/Expertise | Unique Value You Bring
- Role | Helping \_\_\_ (type of company) do \_\_\_ (result)
- I help \_\_\_ (type of company) do \_\_\_ (desired result)
- What you do/how you meet someone’s pain point | Keyword 1 | Keyword 2 | Keyword 3
- <Role/Job Title> specializing in \_\_\_\_, \_\_\_\_, and \_\_\_\_
- X Years of Experience in \_\_\_\_ | Helping companies \_\_\_\_

### **Experiences**

When it comes to writing the experience section, follow the X,Y,Z framework we have mentioned here

1. **X – Context:**  
   Start by providing the context or background of the experience. This sets the stage for understanding the situation. Example:
   - “During my time as a Marketing Intern at ABC Company…”
2. **Y – Your Role/Responsibilities:**  
   Outline your role or responsibilities during the experience. Highlight your main duties and tasks. Example:
   - “I was responsible for creating social media content, analyzing campaign performance, and assisting with event coordination.”
3. **Z – Achievements/Results:**  
   Share any achievements or results that came out of your efforts. Quantify your accomplishments whenever possible. Example:
   - “Increased social media engagement by 25% within the first month, resulting in a broader reach and improved brand visibility.
