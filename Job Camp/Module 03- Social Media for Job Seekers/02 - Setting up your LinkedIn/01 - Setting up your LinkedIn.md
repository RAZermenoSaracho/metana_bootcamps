# Setting up your LinkedIn

### Let’s take a look at some important areas in your Linkedin

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rNwAzTNNn4s>

### **Banner Image on LinkedIn**

The banner image should provide a brief idea about:

1. Who you are – e.g., Your name “Jessica Spencer”
2. What tech you use – Example: “Front-end Developer | React”
3. How to contact you – Example: “jessica@email.com”

- You can even include the logos of the technologies and programming languages you’re skilled at on your banner image.

### **Profile Picture**

You absolutely need to have a profile picture. It should be a clear picture of your happy, decent-looking face. You can also include a video about yourself in the profile picture section.

Why is the profile picture important?

There are two sets of interviews: the first is an interview aimed at understanding what kind of person you are and to determine if you’re a happy, decent person with a professional attitude. By including a proper, decent profile picture and maybe a good video, you can give a better impression about yourself to the recruiter.

### **Headline**

The headline must immediately show your value and give an idea of what they will come across on your profile.

The headline can include the roles you want to get into, e.g., Blockchain Developer, along with your top 3 technical skills that blockchain developers require. Make sure your headline and the banner image are in line, so you can confirm your skills in both these places.

### **About Section**

Put the most valuable information that recruiters want to know first, so they’ll keep reading your profile in-depth.

Get more in-depth on what technologies you use right now and mention the skills you’re currently learning. This is because you may not always tick all the boxes required by the recruiter, but you can show them that you’re on the road to learning them.

**Let’s take an example:**

Imagine your recruiter is searching for Web3 developers with smart contract development and security audit skills. At this point, you have as in-depth knowledge in smart contracts as same as the other candidates, but you’re the only person who has said that you’re currently learning security auditing. This is a powerful and great opportunity to stand out from 100s of applicants to grab the attention of recruiters.

**The About Section Guideline:**

- What are the technologies you know
- What are the technologies you’re learning
- Where can we see proof of your work

### **The LinkedIn Contact Info**

Let’s imagine a recruiter who found your LinkedIn profile and decided to interview you. It’s important that you provide multiple methods of communication. The contact methods can include access to your portfolio site, GitHub link, and your email address.
