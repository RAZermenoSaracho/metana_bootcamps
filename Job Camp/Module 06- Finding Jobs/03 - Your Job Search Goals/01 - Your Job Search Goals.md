# Your Job Search Goals

#####

## Overview

We believe in ?quality over quantity? as a search tactic. Speaking of tactics, let’s clarify your search goals. To simplify your focus, we encourage you to aim for a weekly search goal of 10-10-10.

**10 Quality Applications**

Instead of randomly applying here and there, focus on submitting 10 well-researched and tailored applications per week. By targeting opportunities that align with your goals, you increase your chances of securing interview invitations.

**10 Outreach Messages**

Don’t underestimate the power of networking and outreach. Alongside your applications, send out 10 personalized messages to professionals in your desired field. Building connections and bringing attention to your candidacy can significantly enhance your job search.

**10 Technical Skill-building Activities**

To stay sharp and confident in interviews, dedicate time to improving your technical skills. Engage in 10 skill-building activities per week, such as coding challenges, projects, or online courses. This continuous learning will demonstrate your dedication and expertise to potential employers.

The 10-10-10 approach is designed to maximize your job search effectiveness. It’s essential to remember that follow-up is crucial after applying and reaching out. Once you secure an interview, your preparation in technical skills and behavioral interview practice will shine through.

## Why 10-10-10?

Cold outreach messages and applications typically receive a response rate of around 10-30%. Therefore, randomly applying here and there without proper follow-up is unlikely to yield the desired outcome of securing interview invitations, which ultimately lead to job offers.

The 10-10-10 approach simplifies the most effective job search action: apply for an opportunity and follow up. Proactive outreach is crucial to draw attention to your candidacy and increase your chances of obtaining interviews.

By completing follow-ups, you create opportunities to secure interview invitations. To excel in these interviews, ensure you stay sharp by engaging in 10 skill-building activities per week to enhance your technical expertise.
