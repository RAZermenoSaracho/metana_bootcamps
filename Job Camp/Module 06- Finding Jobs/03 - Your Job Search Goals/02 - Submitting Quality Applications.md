# Submitting Quality Applications

## Overview

Job searching can be exhausting, especially when you don’t hear back from companies.

Some job seekers take a quantity approach to their search, submitting applications through “one-click” or “easy apply”, you upload your resume and call it a day. The act of clicking submit might feel productive in the moment, but days or weeks later this approach doesn’t often produce the desired result - an invitation to an interview. You might as well call this approach the application or resume black hole.

If you want a higher response rate on applications, you need to:

1) Submit a quality application, then…  
2) Follow-up on that application by reaching out to a human.

### Quality Applications ✅

What makes an application a quality application ✨

→ **Apply to a posting that has been live within the past 2 weeks.** ⏰

The newer a posting is, the more likely it’s still in the review stages. After 2+ business weeks, the likelihood applications have already been reviewed increases. If something was posted in the last 24 hours, try to complete the application ASAP so you’re one of the first candidates in the pool. ?

→ **Apply directly through a company’s website**. 🌐

As you discover opportunities you want to apply for through job boards, take a few minutes to look up the open role on that company’s website. This helps you discover more information about the company or role. This is also the most direct method of applying – it lands right inside a company’s ATS, removing intermediary websites used to discover the opportunity.

What if the role isn’t posted on their website? Then it’s okay to use the platform you discovered it through to apply! Remember, some job boards are web crawlers so it’s possible the job board is showing you a dated posting. ?️

→ **Have 50% (or more) of the requirements or preferred qualifications.**✅

A job description is a company’s wishlist – it’s the perfect candidate. The perfect candidate rarely exists, and most companies consider strong candidates. Don’t get hung up on meeting 100% of the job requirements for a role. Cultural fit, team dynamics, and an openness to learn are aspects companies prioritize as they interview candidates. A company can’t invite you to interview if you eliminate yourself as a candidate, so, APPLY! If you meet half of the requirements on a job description, throw your name in the hat – let them decide if they want to remove you from the candidate pool. For the requirements you do have, emphasize them by…

**→ Use a strong and relevant resume to apply 🚀**

The first few times you apply for open roles you may find yourself tweaking the resume you made in Labs, but don’t get too caught up making tweaks to your resume every time you apply. Instead, make strong resume versions that can be reused for similar role types. For example, as a Web Developer, you may find yourself with a full-stack resume and a back-end one that emphasizes related skills slightly differently. Each version should capture the keywords and skills that qualify you for related roles. To understand what employers are looking for in a strong and relevant resume, here is a great video tutorial of a resume workshop hosted by Bloomberg.

If you hit all the above points on an application, it’s almost quality. You’re just missing one crucial step that we will cover in the next module.

## Next Steps

Use the interested column in your job tracker to pick an opportunity to apply for before the end of today. Then, ensure that opportunity hits all of the following boxes:

☑️ You meet at least half (50%) of the qualifications

☑️ Job posting is less than 2 weeks old ⏰

☑️ Applying directly to the company using their website ?

☑️ Use a resume that emphasizes keywords and skills needed for the job ?

By following these steps, you increase your chances of getting noticed and receiving responses from potential employers. Good luck!
