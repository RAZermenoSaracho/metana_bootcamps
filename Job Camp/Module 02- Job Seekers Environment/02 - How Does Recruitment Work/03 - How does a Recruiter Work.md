# How does a Recruiter Work

If you want to understand how a recruiter works in a very simple manner, here’s how it happens; Recruiters give you **10 minutes of their time** to see if your a match, so first impression matters a lot

**Types of Recruiters**

- Corporate recruiter
- Talent acquisition specialist
- Employment representative
- Personnel coordinator
- Human resources specialist
- Personnel officer

### **So where do recruiters go on the hunt for potential candidates -**

- Attendance at blockchain conferences
- Crypto forums, GitHub, Reddit, Discord channels, and Telegram groups.
- Hackathons
- Meetups is also common for recruiters
- Most importantly LinkedIn
- Sometimes on X as well ( formerly known as Twitter )
