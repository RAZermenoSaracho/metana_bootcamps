# Different Types of Profile Reading

As we mentioned earlier, we need to get the recruiters attention to your CV as quickest as possible . Recruiters look at your profile in three layers:

✅ **Layer 1: This is the first few items that the recruiter sees on your profile and decides to mark for further reading.**  
✅ **Layer 2: The selected profiles from Layer 1 are scan-read, and the ones that don’t fit the recruiter’s checklist are removed.**  
✅ **Layer 3: The selected profiles from Layer 2 would then be interviewed by the recruiter.**

An important point to remember is that you don’t progress to Layers 2 and 3 if you fail in Layer 1. In order to move up the layers, the previous layers must be passed systematically.

To pass Layer 1, developers need to show evidence of their skills and knowledge at first glance and tick all the boxes on the recruiter’s checklist. After that, developers can seal the deal. Luckily, you have LinkedIn to solve this for you, and we will inform you of the step-by-step process on how to do this in the upcoming videos.
