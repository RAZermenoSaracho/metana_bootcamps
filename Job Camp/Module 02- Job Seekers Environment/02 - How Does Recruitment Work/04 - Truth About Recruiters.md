# Truth About Recruiters

### **<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oafoUVjGV1M>**

### **Getting a lot of attention from recruiters**

Recruiters don’t work for you, they work to get enough candidates  for the employers, which makes them spam each and every loosely qualified dev to the job role. The real deal is not how many recruiters reach out to you but the amount of interviews you have with these recruiters

### **Recruiters are not technical**

Recruiters are not technical (most of the time) they don’t have knowledge on programming or other technical expertise as you. All what they care is to find the right candidate suitable for their client

What recruiters do have is a checklist that can analyze if the candidate is fit for the role or not. Therefore if you have the right skills make sure

1. Your skills are visible for recruiters as soon as they check your CV or linkedin
2. You inspire confidence for the recruiters to choose you out of all other candidates
3. Finally make sure you do both of the above two points quickly as possible

### **The best developers rarely get the Job**

So, you think you’re the best developer for the job?  
Guess what? , Everyone says that, and recruiters knows this very well !!  
That’s why they prefer proof of work.

**What does this mean?**  
You need to demonstrate that you have completed projects showcasing the skills required for the job. For example, if a recruiter is looking for React expertise, present a GitHub repo of your best React projects. If they want to see your smart contract development skills, show them the contracts you’ve created.

Always provide proof of your work. It’s not just the best developers who get the job; it’s the ones who can effectively present their skills.

### **Keep Recruiters Reading**

Provide sufficient value in your profile to motivate recruiters to continue reading. This includes giving them a reason to read your profile in depth and consider you as a potential interview candidate. To achieve this, we need to make your profile compelling enough for recruiters to want to learn more, similar to swiping right on Tinder, and help you rank in the top 10 of their candidate list.

### **Recruiters are Lazy**

Time is the biggest enemy of recruiters. You need to communicate your value as efficiently and effectively as possible on your LinkedIn profile. This means you have to utilize the tools available to you on LinkedIn, such as the Banner image, Headline, and About section, etc.

The more value you provide, the more attention you’ll receive from recruiters. If your LinkedIn profile can tick off all the requirements expected for the job role as quickly as possible for the recruiter, within 10 minutes, this can open doors to more opportunities for you in the market.
