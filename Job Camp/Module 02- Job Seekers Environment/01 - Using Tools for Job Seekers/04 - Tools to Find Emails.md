### Why use Hunter.io ?

[Hunter.io](http://hunter.io/) is super handy for job seekers. It helps you find and verify email addresses of hiring managers, recruiters, and key people at companies you’re interested in. This means you can reach out directly, follow up on your applications, and send personalized emails. It’s great for networking, cold emailing for job opportunities, and building your professional network, making it easier to land job opportunities and get career advice.

### Using Apollo.io

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/8b/4a/8b4a9a4ea44b8e749064e299bcbde7288a227b1367a6b0c727a93566d0220366.png)

[Apollo.io](http://apollo.io/) is a great tool for job seekers. This extension helps you find and verify email addresses of key contacts at companies, making it easier to reach out directly. You can use it to discover potential leads, automate your email outreach, and track interactions.

You can then use <http://apollo.io/> to filter out those companies. You can also get direct contact to people who work there if you use the appropriate filters.
