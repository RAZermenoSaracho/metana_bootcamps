[Careerflow.ai](http://careerflow.ai/) is a revolutionary job tracking tool and CRM that empowers you and your organization to effectively manage your job search and in the future - overall career journey.

In addition to job tracking, It offers a plethora of innovative features to optimize the career search process. Utilizing AI-powered resume reviews and LinkedIn profile optimization, identifying skill gaps, and providing tailored career guidance, Careerflow equips you with cutting-edge tools to elevate your career outcomes.

When using job boards, here are some tips to keep in mind:

⏰ Set your filters to `Date Posted = last 24 hours` or `last week`. Avoid using additional filters such as “entry-level” or “years of experience required,” as these can be unreliable.

Use skill-based keywords instead of role titles. For example, use terms like `React`, `HTML/CSS`, `JavaScript`, and `Python` to refine your search.
