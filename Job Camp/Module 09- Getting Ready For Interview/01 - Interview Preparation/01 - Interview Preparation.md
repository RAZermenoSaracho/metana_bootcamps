# Interview Preparation

Let’s watch this video to get an idea of how you should be prepared to face an interview

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hSHoFV_cjSg>

The best way to prepare for an interview is to gather as much information as possible about the company and the job role. You can connect with your recruiter to request details about the role and the company. Another creative method is to research the company and its employees on social media platforms. LinkedIn, in particular, provides an overview of the projects currently underway at the company, helping you understand how your knowledge could apply to these projects.

Another important aspect of interview preparation is to research the skills of current employees in the role you’ve applied for. This can be done by searching for the job role in the “People” section of the company’s LinkedIn profile and examining the skills mentioned on their profile.
