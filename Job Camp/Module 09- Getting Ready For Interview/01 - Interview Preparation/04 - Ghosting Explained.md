# Ghosting Explained

Being ghosted is tough and awful. The wisest thing to do if you’ve been ghosted is to avoid complaining about it on social media. Although ghosting is unpleasant, posting about how you have been ghosted by companies can cast a negative light on your capabilities and skills.

### Here’s why you’ve been ghosted

Recruitment is a speed game, which means recruiters need to find a suitable candidate as quickly as possible for their client. This involves reaching out to as many candidates as possible and selecting the best one.

Recruiters might interview or approach you to clarify if you possess the specific skills required for the job role. If you weren’t able to successfully present yourself as a potential candidate with the necessary skills at this stage, this could be a possible reason for being ghosted.

After you have been ghosted, try to understand the reason and work to improve your LinkedIn profile and your skills to better fit the roles you are applying for.
