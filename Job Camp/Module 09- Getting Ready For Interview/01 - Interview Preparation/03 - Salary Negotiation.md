# Salary Negotiation

Negotiating your salary can be intimidating, but you don’t need to worry if you have a well-planned negotiation strategy

### Let’s take a look at this Video

[Video: How to Negotiate Salary: Asking for More Money After a Job Offer | Indeed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xap454_Nq2g)

The salary expectation ranges from person to person, but we all want to get a fair salary, right?

Getting the upper end of the salary sounds like the best deal, but let’s say you want to step foot inside the company, and asking for the highest end of the salary seems pretty risky.

There’s a way to approach this: you can negotiate a contract where you start with an entry-level salary and ask the company what you should achieve in terms of performance and results to achieve the higher end of the salary.

Having this plan in place can improve your chances of achieving the higher end of the salary.
