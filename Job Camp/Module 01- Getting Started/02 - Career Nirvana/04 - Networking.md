# Networking

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zknO3GkSFAg>

Building a network of contacts in your desired industry or field is an important step in advancing your career. This could involve:

- Attending industry events or conferences to meet people in the field.
- Joining professional organizations or online communities related to your field.
- Reaching out to people in your network who work in the field you’re interested in.
- Participating in online forums or discussion groups related to your field.

> **By building a network of contacts, you’ll be able to learn more about your desired career path, get advice and guidance from experienced professionals, and potentially discover new job opportunities. ?? Expanding your network allows you to tap into the knowledge and experiences of others, gaining valuable insights and mentorship along the way. It can open doors to new opportunities and help you navigate your career path with greater confidence and clarity.**
