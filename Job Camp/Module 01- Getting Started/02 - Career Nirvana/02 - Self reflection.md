# Self reflection

## A fulfilling Career

In order to find a fulfilling career, it’s important to first understand yourself and what you want out of your professional life. This could involve asking yourself questions such as:

- What are my values, and how do they relate to my work?
- What are my strengths and skills, and how can I apply them in my career?
- What are my interests and passions, and how can I incorporate them into my work?
- What are my short-term and long-term career goals, and how can I achieve them?

## Pro tip

🚀 The man who tries to catch two rabbits, catches none.  
  
🚀 Tip: Stick with one skillset (don’t go for a CSM role while also going for a PM role, too)  
  
🚀 Tip: The ONLY time you can go after two different roles is if/when there is an overlap between the two roles, such as Project Management and Program Management

**Remember, self-reflection is to discovering a career that brings you joy and satisfaction!**
