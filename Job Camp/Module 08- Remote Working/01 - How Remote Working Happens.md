# How Remote Working Happens

### **Changes in Time Zones**

Companies have a fear of going remote due to different time zones. The more different the time zone is between you and the hiring company, the more fear the company has when employing you. This is why many companies favor local employees and employees with less different time zones.

However, that doesn’t mean you can’t land remote roles. Companies still seek remote workers due to their technical expertise and cost efficiency.

### **Salary and Leverage in Remote Working**

If you’re a developer living outside the US or Europe, you might find that earning in foreign currency can provide a higher standard of living than earning in local currency. This can be a leverage point, as you can work for the lower end of the salary scale and still make a substantial amount of money in local currency due to exchange rate benefits.

However, let’s say you want to earn the same salary as a developer in San Francisco. This means you are competing with all other developers in San Francisco. Therefore, you must prove to the recruiter or employer that your skills and expertise are so exceptional that they should choose you over all the local developers and still pay you a higher salary.

### **How to identify the Skills for Remote Work**

When becoming a remote worker, it is crucial not only to know the skills but to become a technical expert in them. The best method to ensure you have all the relevant skills is to go through all the major job boards and analyze the most common skills that these jobs require. Once you review multiple job postings, you will notice there is always a common set of tech stacks required.

So what’s next? Now that you have identified the skills, it’s time to present evidence that you are a technical expert in these skills and secure your remote job.

### **Appearing Professional**

You might prefer remote working because it allows you to travel around the world while still working.  
However, your employer may be concerned about your productivity. Therefore, when you participate in an interview or a meeting, ensure you are in a setting ( background ) that looks professional.

This demonstrates to your employer that you take your work seriously and remain productive during working hours
