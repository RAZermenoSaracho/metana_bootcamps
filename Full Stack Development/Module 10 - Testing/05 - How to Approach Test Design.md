# How to Approach Test Design

Designing your tests is as crucial as planning the structure of your software. Remember, tests are code, and they should meet the same quality standards as your main codebase.

Key principles for writing effective tests

- **Clarity**: Make your tests easy to understand for anyone reading them.
- **Documentation**: Provide clear summaries at the top of your test files and add explanatory comments where necessary.
- **Repeatability**: Ensure that each test, when given the same inputs, produces the same result consistently.
- **Isolation**: Tests should be independent of each other and not rely on the state of other tests or parts of your software.

Approaching test design with these principles in mind ensures that your tests are reliable, maintainable, and effective in verifying the functionality of your software.
