# TDD Vs. BDD

- *TDD*: Focuses on testing the functionality of individual units or components of the software.
- *BDD (Behavior-driven development)*: Focuses on testing the behavior of the software from the user’s perspective. Tests are written in a more human-readable format, emphasizing the behavior of the system rather than the implementation details.
