# Revenue Models

- When you decide to build a commercial product, service, or other business, at some point you have to figure out how it will earn you money.
- There are many different systems used to generate money from a product, besides just selling it for a fixed price. These different models of earning income are called **revenue models**.
- Defining your **revenue model** is the primary way you get from an “idea on the web” to “profitable business”.

### **What is a revenue model ?**

- A strategy outlining how a business generates income.
- Describes how a company earns revenue from its products or services.

### **Types of revenue model for software startups**

1. **Subscription model**: Users pay a recurring fee for access to the software.
2. **Freemium model**: Basic features are free, but premium features require payment.
3. **Advertising model**: Revenue is generated through displaying ads within the software.
4. **Transaction fee model**: Revenue is earned by taking a percentage of transactions facilitated through the software.
5. **Licensing model**: Other companies pay for the right to use the software.

### **Choosing the right model for your product idea**

- Understand your target market and their willingness to pay.
- Consider the nature of your software and how users interact with it — figure out what they would want this product to do, and how much value that is worth to them.
- Look at successful companies doing a similar product to your own, and see what models they are using.
- Define your revenue model clearly — understand exactly how you intend to earn money — but, don’t be afraid to evolve your profit model over time.
