# Collecting Ideas

- We all have tons of ideas — collecting and refining them is key.
- You need a **central place** for idea collection, and tasks.
  - Use Notion, or a similar organization tool — but whatever you use, you need to have only one place for everything.
  - Scattered throughout multiple notebooks, mobile apps, websites, and documents doesn’t help you achieve anything.
  - Any ideas you have for new projects, half-formed ideas or complete business plans, should go a central “project ideas” list.
  - Capture your creative ideas, and review them later.
- Organize your projects and todo actions, using a notes application and the PARA system
  - Projects folder
    - Create a top-level Projects directory *(P)* — all your *projects* go there.
    - Each project gets its own sub-folder.
    - Anything that’s inspiration for your project ideas, questions, notes related to the project should be collected there.
  - Daily to-dos and planning
    - Project actions are small, bite-sized steps you can perform in a few minutes or a couple hours.
    - These need to be kept in an actions list, and moved to a planner.
    - Collect actionable items into a daily schedule & todo list — incorporate it into your daily planner.
