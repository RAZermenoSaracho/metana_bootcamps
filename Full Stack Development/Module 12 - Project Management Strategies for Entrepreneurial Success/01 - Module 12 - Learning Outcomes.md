# Module 12 : Learning Outcomes

[YouTube video player](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8lhJqPd_ws)

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of project management and entrepreneurship.
- Apply development sprint methodologies to manage and execute projects effectively.
- Develop and market a viable business idea, from ideation to execution.

### **Unit 124: Intro to Project Management**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Explain the purpose and principles of project management.
- Identify the key roles and responsibilities in project management.
- Understand how project management frameworks contribute to successful outcomes.

### **Unit 125: Software Development Methodologies**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Compare common software development methodologies, including Agile and Waterfall.
- Understand the benefits and drawbacks of each methodology in real-world scenarios.
- Choose an appropriate methodology for a given project context.

### **Unit 126: Intro to Development Sprints**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Define a development sprint and its purpose in project management.
- Explain the key components of a sprint, including planning, execution, and review.
- Participate effectively in sprint-based project workflows.

### **Unit 127: Issue Tracking**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Utilize issue tracking tools to monitor project progress.
- Identify and prioritize issues within a project.
- Collaborate effectively to resolve tracked issues.

### **Unit 128: Working With A Project Manager**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Understand the role and responsibilities of a project manager.
- Collaborate with a project manager to achieve project goals.
- Communicate effectively to provide updates and feedback during a project.

### **Unit 129: Intro to Entrepreneurship**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Define entrepreneurship and its core concepts.
- Identify the characteristics of successful entrepreneurs.
- Explain the challenges and rewards of starting a business.

### **Unit 130: Entrepreneurship Vs. Employment**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Compare the advantages and challenges of entrepreneurship and employment.
- Evaluate personal goals and preferences to decide between career paths.
- Understand how entrepreneurship and employment can complement each other.

### **Unit 131: Entrepreneurship Mindset**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Develop a growth-oriented mindset for entrepreneurial success.
- Recognize and address common fears and challenges in entrepreneurship.
- Cultivate resilience and adaptability as an entrepreneur.

### **Unit 132: Information Systems**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Understand the role of information systems in modern businesses.
- Identify key components of an effective information system.
- Leverage information systems for decision-making and efficiency.

### **Unit 133: Defining Marketable Ideas**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Brainstorm and evaluate potential business ideas.
- Identify the characteristics of a marketable idea.
- Validate ideas through research and feedback.

### **Unit 134: MVPs and Signup Pages**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Define a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and its role in startups.
- Design and deploy effective signup pages for user acquisition.
- Gather and analyze user feedback to iterate on your MVP.

### **Unit 135: Pitch Decks**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Create a professional and compelling pitch deck.
- Organize key information to present your business idea effectively.
- Tailor pitch decks to different audiences, including investors and customers.

### **Unit 136: How to Make a Convincing Pitch**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Develop key skills for delivering a persuasive pitch.
- Handle common objections and questions during a pitch.
- Engage your audience with confidence and clarity.

### **Unit 137: “Getting Investment” 101**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Understand the fundamentals of securing investment for a business.
- Identify different types of funding sources, including angel investors and venture capital.
- Prepare for and navigate investor meetings successfully.

### **Unit 138: Revenue Models**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Identify various revenue models and their applications.
- Choose a revenue model suitable for your business idea.
- Analyze and optimize revenue streams for profitability.

### **Unit 139: Development Sprints**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Plan and execute development sprints effectively.
- Coordinate team efforts during sprint cycles.
- Reflect and iterate on sprint outcomes to improve processes.

### **Unit 140: Marketing Your Ideas**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Develop a marketing strategy tailored to your target audience.
- Leverage digital and traditional marketing channels effectively.
- Measure the impact of your marketing efforts to refine your approach.

### **Unit 141: Making Your First Sale, and Beyond**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Identify strategies to acquire your first customers.
- Build long-term relationships with customers to ensure retention.
- Scale sales processes to support business growth.

### **Unit 142: Building Your Real-World Project**

**Learning Outcomes**

- Apply project management, development, and marketing skills to build a real-world project.
- Present your project to stakeholders or customers confidently.
- Reflect on lessons learned to enhance future entrepreneurial ventures.
