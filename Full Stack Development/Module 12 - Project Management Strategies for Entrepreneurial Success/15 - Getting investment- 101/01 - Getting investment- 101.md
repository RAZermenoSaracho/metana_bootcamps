# "Getting investment" 101

### **What does it mean to “get funding” ?**

- While as web engineers we all love the idea of bootstrapping our own businesses with nothing but hustle and coding skills, sometimes that’s just not enough.
- If you want to accelerate your idea from a “garage project” to “multimillion dollar unicorn” status, you’re going to need ***funding***.
- Funding is **investment in your company**, to help it accelerate its growth.
- It's used to help you advertise and market to customers, purchase infrastructure, pay for other engineers to accelerate development, and even pay for yourself to focus full-time on your project.

### **Where does it come from ?**

- Investment can come from various sources — from your own pocket to million-dollar **seed investments** from venture capitalists.
- Some investment sources include:
  - Crowdfunding platforms like [Kickstarter.com](http://kickstarter.com/), Indiegogo, and Gofundme.
  - Personal savings, using your own money to kickstart your business.
  - Gifts and loans from friends and family who want to support your project or be a part of it.
  - Investors, including venture capitalists and “angel investors” who expect returns from their investments.
  - Business loans from banks, although they are less open-minded than venture capitalist investors.

### **What’s the catch ?**

- When you take funding from an investor, it’s never “free”.
- Investors will expect some part of ownership in your company.
- Don’t take the first offer that comes along — the investor might try to snatch up a great idea cheaply for a major ownership share. If you don’t like the offer, you can say “no thank you!” and shop around for a better one.
