# Intro to Pitch Decks

When we’re pitching ideas to potential customers, investors, supporters, or other stakeholders, one of the most powerful tools you have is called a pitch deck.

**What is a Pitch Deck ?**

- A presentation comprised of slides.
- It outlines a business idea, product, or service.
- Commonly utilized by entrepreneurs or startups to attract investors or partners.

**What is it for ?**

- Conveying essential information succinctly.
- Generating interest and excitement about the venture.
- Persuading potential investors or partners to take action.

**Basic Rules of Pitch Decks**

- Keep it concise: Typically 10-15 slides.
- Focus on visuals: Utilize images, charts, and graphs.
- Tell a compelling story: Cover the problem, solution, market, team, and traction.
- Practice delivery: Ensure clarity and confidence during presentation.

**Tools for Building Your Pitch Deck**

- PowerPoint: Traditional software with extensive features.
- Keynote: Mac-specific tool known for its sleek designs.
- Google Slides: Collaborative platform accessible from any device.
- Canva: User-friendly platform offering pre-designed templates.
- [Pitch.com](http://pitch.com/): Advanced tool with interactive features for modern presentations.
