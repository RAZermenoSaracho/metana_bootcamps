# Track Your Progress

### **Intro to Project Tracking**

- Project tracking is as crucial as the planning phase itself. It ensures that the project stays on course and progress aligns with expectations.
- Utilize project management tools like **Kanban boards** to visually track progress towards milestones. This provides a clear overview of tasks and their current status within the sprint.

### **Milestones**

- Milestones represent significant achievements or stages in a development plan. Each milestone should have a **projected date** to create time pressure for completion.
- Break down milestones into manageable tasks, ensuring clarity and focus on specific objectives.

### **Tasks and Sub-tasks**

- Tasks are major steps towards achieving a milestone. If a task requires more than 1-2 days of work, it should be divided into **sub-tasks** for better manageability.
- Sub-tasks allow for a more granular breakdown of work, making it easier to track progress and identify potential bottlenecks.

### **Moving Tasks**

- As work progresses, tasks should be moved through different stages of completion, such as "backlog," "in progress," "ready for review," "needs revision," and "done." This ensures transparency and accountability within the team.
