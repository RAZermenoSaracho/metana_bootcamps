# Intro to Development Sprints

### What are sprints ?

- Sprints in software development are iterative cycles of work where a specific set of tasks or features are planned, developed, and delivered within a fixed timeframe, typically ranging from one to four weeks.
- Each sprint aims to produce a potentially shippable increment of the product, allowing for continuous feedback and adaptation.
- Sprints enable rapid iteration, enabling teams to respond quickly to changes and deliver value incrementally throughout the development process.

### Why use sprints ?

- Sprints create artificial time pressure, pushing you to get a measurable feature built or task accomplished, in a given amount of time.
- They allow you to build a timeline, composed of small individual feature sprints, letting you measure your progress against the entire project development timeline.
