# Tips on How To Make a Convincing Pitch

**Pitching an idea** — means telling someone about your product or service, with the goal of **getting them to buy it / pay you for it / invest in it**.

- **Pitching ideas** may be to employers, customers, or investors. if you’re pitching to an employer, this might be presenting your project idea with the goal of **getting them to allow you to develop it**.
- Pitching your ideas is ***marketing*** — even if you’re just telling your friend or coworker about it.
- This can be formal or informal presentation, but telling people about your project is part of the process.
- We use **pitch decks for formal presentations**, typically for investment. It’s a powerful tool that lets you **hone and refine your message**.

### Making it Convincing

- Your pitch needs to be **convincing** — make the person believe in it as much as you do.
- When presenting ideas, **assume the audience has no idea what the product is**, doesn’t know what terms you’re using, doesn’t know why it would matter, and doesn’t know what problem it would solve, or who would pay for it.
- Finally, **why should they (the audience) want to pay you for it or otherwise support or invest in it** ?
- You have to inform them of all of these things, and **convince them of each**.
- **Important points**:
  1. **Explain clearly and simply what your product is**
  2. **Explain terms and jargon** — don’t assume they know
  3. **Tell them why it matters** — what is this good for ? Why is it important **now** ?
  4. **What problem would it solve** ?
  5. **Who would pay for it** ?
  6. **Why should this person give you [money / funding / resources / support] for it** ?
- **Tell a story** — stories are more convincing than dry reasoning and sales pitches. Tell the story of who uses your product, what their life was like before, what their problem was, how they then use your producte, and how their life has been improved by using it.
- **Use data** — data and graphs will drive home the reality of your concept, making it something more than “just an idea”.
