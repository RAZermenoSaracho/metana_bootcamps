# Can you really build a startup in your spare time ?

- It's common to have a full-time job, but how do you find the time and energy to start a business alongside it ? Is it even possible ?
- Surprisingly, you can create a successful startup by dedicating just 2 hours a day, provided you stay focused.
- Begin by committing one hour each day to your startup efforts, then gradually increase as needed.
