### **Introduction to Kanban Boards**

Kanban boards are visual tools used to manage and track work in progress. Originating from lean manufacturing principles, Kanban boards have become popular in project management for their simplicity and effectiveness.

### **How Kanban Boards Aid in Project Management** ?

Kanban boards provide a clear visualization of workflow, allowing teams to see tasks at a glance and understand their status. They promote transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement by making work visible and limiting work in progress.

### **Implementing and Managing Kanban Boards**

To implement and manage Kanban boards effectively, teams should define workflow stages, set WIP (work in progress) limits, and prioritize tasks. Regular review meetings help identify bottlenecks and optimize the flow of work.
