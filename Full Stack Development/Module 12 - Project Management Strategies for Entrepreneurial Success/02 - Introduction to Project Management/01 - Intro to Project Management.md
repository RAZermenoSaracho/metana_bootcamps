# Intro to Project Management

### What is Project Management ?

Project management is like being the captain of a ship. It's about planning, organizing, and guiding a project from start to finish. Think of it as a roadmap that helps everyone involved know what needs to be done, when it needs to be done, and how to do it.

### Why is Project Management critical in software development ?

In software development, there are lots of moving parts. Project management keeps everything on track. It helps teams stay organized, meet deadlines, and deliver high-quality products. Without it, projects can become chaotic, leading to missed deadlines, budget overruns, and unhappy clients.

### Key Roles in Project Management

In the world of project management, there are a few key players,

- **Project Manager**: The leader who oversees the entire project. They're like the captain of the ship, steering the team towards success.
- **Team Members**: These are the people doing the work. They could be developers, designers, testers, or other specialists.
- **Stakeholders**: These are the people who have an interest in the project's outcome. It could be the client, investors, or anyone else affected by the project.
- **Clients or Customers**: The people who will ultimately use the product or service being developed.

### Project Management Systems

There are different systems and tools that project managers use to keep everything running smoothly. These systems help with things like scheduling, task assignment, communication, and tracking progress. Some popular project management systems include Trello, Asana, Jira, and Microsoft Project. Each system has its own features and benefits, so it's important to choose one that works best for your team and project.
