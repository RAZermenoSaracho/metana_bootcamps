# Assignment M12

This assignment is designed to simulate a real-world development sprint, allowing you to apply both project management strategies and technical development skills. Whether working individually or in a group, you’ll choose a project that aligns with your interests and abilities, then guide it through structured phases, from ideation and planning to execution and review. The ultimate goal is to produce a project that demonstrates your expertise and readiness for future professional opportunities, while also helping you build a strong portfolio.

You’ll be working with tools like *GitHub* for collaboration and *ClickUp* task management, presenting your progress at various stages, and engaging in peer and instructor feedback to refine your work. Whether you’re tackling an individual project or collaborating in a group, this assignment will help you hone your technical, organizational, and communication skills.

***You will work on either a group or individual project. Download the appropriate guide based on your choice.***
