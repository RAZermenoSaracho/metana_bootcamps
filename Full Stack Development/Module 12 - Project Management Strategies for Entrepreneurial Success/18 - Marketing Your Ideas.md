# Marketing Your Ideas

### What is marketing ?

- Marking is how we get people to know about our products and businesses. Essentially, it’s advertising and sales combined.
- When you communicate to people about your idea, whether in person, by email, by web campaign or advertisement, you are marketing.
- We aim to let people know our product or service exists, and get them interested in paying you for it.
- Even the website you create is a form of marketing — remember to invest in SEO so that people can find it.

### Who are you marketing to ?

- Identify the target audience for your product, using the user personas method.
- Talk to people who match your ideal customer, and see what they actually think — are they interested ? Do they match your idea ?
- Reach out to people by LinkedIn or other social media, and just ask questions. Be friendly and polite, and people will often be willing to engage.

### Models for marketing

- There are tons of models and methods for marketing — but we can’t cover them all in a full stack crash-course.
- Here are a few models — it’s not important to know them all, just look through the list and find one or two models that are a good fit for your project or business
  1. **Inbound Marketing**
     - Attracting potential customers through valuable content, SEO, and social media.
     - Techniques include blogging, content creation, email marketing, and search engine optimization.
  2. **Content Marketing**
     - Creating and distributing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a defined audience.
     - Utilizes blogs, articles, videos, infographics, and other content formats.
  3. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**
     - Optimizing website content to improve its visibility and ranking on search engine results pages (SERPs).
     - Techniques include keyword research, on-page optimization, link building, and improving website speed and user experience.
  4. **Social Media Marketing**
     - Utilizing social media platforms to build brand awareness, engage with audiences, and drive traffic to the website.
     - Includes creating and sharing content, engaging with followers, running ads, and leveraging influencer partnerships.
  5. **Email Marketing**
     - Building and nurturing relationships with leads and customers through targeted email campaigns.
     - Involves sending personalized and relevant content, promotional offers, newsletters, and automated email sequences.
  6. **Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising**
     - Placing ads on search engines and other websites, paying only when users click on the ad.
     - Techniques include Google Ads, Bing Ads, display advertising, and remarketing campaigns.
  7. **Affiliate Marketing**
     - Partnering with individuals or businesses (affiliates) who promote the startup's products or services in exchange for a commission on sales.
     - Involves setting up an affiliate program, recruiting affiliates, and tracking their performance.
  8. **Growth Hacking**
     - Experimenting with unconventional marketing tactics and strategies to achieve rapid and scalable growth.
     - Techniques may include viral marketing, referral programs, product giveaways, and A/B testing.
  9. **Community Building**
     - Building and nurturing a community of loyal customers and brand advocates.
     - Involves engaging with customers through online forums, social media groups, events, and user-generated content.
  10. **Analytics and Data-Driven Marketing**
      - Using analytics tools to track and analyze marketing performance metrics.
      - Involves making data-driven decisions to optimize marketing campaigns, improve targeting, and enhance ROI.

### Most cost-effective and efficient methods for MVP startups

1. Build a signup page - advertise it with Google AdWords.
2. Cold-calling — contact businesses and individuals directly and ask if they’d be interested in trying your product.
3. Blogs and forums — find avenues where people talk about topics related to your business and post about it there.
4. Social media — Build a social media presence, engage with other communities there, and post content. Content gets views, and views get the word out.
