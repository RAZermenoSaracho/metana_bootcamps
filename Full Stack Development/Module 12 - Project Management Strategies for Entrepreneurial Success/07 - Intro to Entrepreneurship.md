# Intro to Entrepreneurship

### What's Covered in this Unit ?

- Your coding crash course is done - now we’re moving on to business and project management.
- **Entrepreneurship**
  - You will either *work for yourself*, or *work for someone else*
  - Deciding if being an entrepreneur is right for you
- **Project Management**
  - Time management and project management will be critical, regardless if you’re working for yourself or for an employer
- **Organizing Information**
  - How to organize, unify, and structure your information — including projects, resources, time records, and your daily workflow
- **Pitching Ideas**
  - Learning how to present your ideas is invaluable — whether marketing to clients, pitching ideas to investors, or present project proposals to your employer

### What's Next on the Agenda ?

- After you complete this section, you will create, present, and being developing your own project.
- You will initially create a list of project ideas, refine them.
- You will then discuss your ideas with a **Metana** coach, who will help you select the best one.
- After that, you’ll further refine it, develop a pitch, and then present your idea.
- Finally, we will move to the building sprints — where you’ll actually build out your project, with supervision and guidance from an instructor.
