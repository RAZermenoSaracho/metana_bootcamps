# Working With A Project Manager

### **Who is a project manager ?**

- A project manager is like the conductor of an orchestra. They coordinate and oversee all aspects of a project, ensuring it stays on track and meets its objectives.

### **What is their role ?**

- Their role is to plan, organize, and execute projects from start to finish. They define project scope, allocate resources, manage budgets, and communicate with stakeholders.

### **How do you best work with your PM ?**

- Communication is key. Keep your project manager updated on your progress, challenges, and any changes to the project plan. Be proactive in seeking clarification and guidance when needed.

### **What to do when you’re behind schedule, or getting stuck on a task ?**

- Don't panic. Inform your project manager immediately and discuss possible solutions. They can help prioritize tasks, allocate additional resources, or adjust the project timeline if necessary. Collaboration and transparency are crucial in overcoming challenges and keeping the project on track.
