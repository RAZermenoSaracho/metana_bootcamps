# Understanding Entrepreneurship vs. Employment

### **Understanding the Difference**

- **Who's Your Boss ?**
  - Employment: You work for a regular paycheck.
  - Entrepreneurship: You earn based on what you sell.
- **Scaling Your Earnings**
  - Employment: Your income stays the same.
  - Entrepreneurship: You can make more as your sales increase.

### **Figuring Out If It's Right for You**

- **Is Entrepreneurship a Fit ?**
  - Do you need someone to tell you what to do, or are you good at motivating yourself?
  - Can you stick to a plan every day, even when it's tough?
  - Are you okay with not knowing exactly how much you'll earn each month?
  - Do you prefer the excitement of something new or the security of a steady paycheck?

### **Pros and Cons**

- **Pros:**
  - You're your own boss.
  - You can set your own hours.
  - If your sales go up, your earnings can too.
  - There's no limit to how much you can earn.
  - You get to work on something you're passionate about.
  - The journey can be really exciting.
- **Cons:**
  - No one tells you what to do, so you need to motivate yourself.
  - You might not have strict deadlines, which can be tough if you're not good at managing your time.
  - Income can be unpredictable, especially when you're starting out.
  - You won't get a regular paycheck like you would with a job.
  - You're responsible for covering all your business expenses.
  - You have to wear many hats, like being the CEO, tech expert, marketer, accountant, and customer service rep all at once.
