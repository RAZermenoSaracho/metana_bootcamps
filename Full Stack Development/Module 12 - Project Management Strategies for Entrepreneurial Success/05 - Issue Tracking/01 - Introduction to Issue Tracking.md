# Introduction to Issue Tracking

### **What is issue tracking ?**

- **Issue tracking** involves monitoring and managing tasks, bugs, or enhancements throughout their lifecycle. It provides a centralized system for recording, prioritizing, and resolving issues that arise during the development process.
  - **Feature tracking**, on the other hand, focuses specifically on tracking the development of new features or functionality within a software project. It involves documenting the requirements, progress, and status of each feature from ideation to implementation. Feature tracking helps ensure that new features are properly planned, developed, and delivered to meet user needs and project goals.

### **Why do we need this as a solo developer ?**

- As a solo developer, issue tracking helps you stay organized and focused by keeping track of tasks, bugs, and ideas. It ensures nothing falls through the cracks and helps you prioritize work effectively.

### **Issue tracking in open-source projects**

- In open-source projects, issue tracking serves as a communication channel between contributors and users. It allows for collaboration, bug reporting, and feature requests, fostering community engagement and continuous improvement.
