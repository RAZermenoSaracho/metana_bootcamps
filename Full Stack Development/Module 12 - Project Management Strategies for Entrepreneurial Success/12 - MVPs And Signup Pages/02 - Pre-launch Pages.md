# Pre-launch Pages

- When you’re working to test an idea quickly, one of the best tools is a “coming soon” page or pre-launch signup page.
- This is a simple **single-page website** with an **email signup form**.
- It introduces what your project ***will be***, and allows interested customers to sign up for notification email when it launches
- Why build a “coming soon” page before you have your project completed ?
  1. **Build momentum** pre-launch — so that when you launch, you already have customers ready to go
  2. **Validate customer interest** — this is a tool to allow you to gauge the level of interest in a product, before you actually build it. This allows you to avoid spending time on a project, if there doesn’t seem to be a strong market interest.
  3. **Iterate quickly** — Release your product idea in one day with a simple launch page. If you don’t gather a strong response, iterate your idea, and try again.
  4. You can even **pre-order** — have customer pay for your product while you’re still building it. This can help you fund your project development.

Tips:

- Use no-code tools for releasing a quick page — don’t spend too much time building it.
- Use a countdown timer to generate a sense of excitement.
- Include a straightforward pitch about what your project is, why it will be awesome, and a prominent signup form for an email list.
- You can describe what you aspire for your project to be: it doesn’t matter if you already have the ability or resources to *build* those features.
