# Introduction to MVPs And Signup Pages

**Intro to MVPs**

- Minimum Viable Product — this is the absolute bare minimum version of a product, that would accomplish its intended goal.
- No frills, extra features, doesn’t need to be polished — just the bare minimum so that people could start using it.
- We use the MVP as a quick way to get started with an idea and gain traction without investing months or years in development
