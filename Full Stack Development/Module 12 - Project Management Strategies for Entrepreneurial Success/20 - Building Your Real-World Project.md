# Building Your Real-World Project

- At this point, you’ve completed this lesson, and completed your course.
- From here, you’re going to go implement all the ideas from this lesson and build your own project.
- Your task:
  1. Ideate, refine, choose, plan, and pitch a project idea.
  2. Build that project to life in a sprint over the next four weeks.

**Ideation phase**

- Ideate — collect 3-5 project ideas.
- Refine — improve those raw ideas into clear projects.
- Choose — discuss your ideas with a Metana coach, and together the two of you will choose the best project idea from your list.
- Plan — write it out into a plan, with goals, features, target market, user story.

Pitch — create a pitch deck — and the present your ideas to your Metana coaching team.

**Development phase**

- Plan it — Plan out your development phases with your project manager (your instructor).
- Build it — Over four weeks, you will sprint towards milestones to build your project.
- Review it — Your project manager will help you review and ensure you’re meeting milestones, and help you pace the development.
- Complete it — Aim to complete your project to a working MVP by the end of your four weeks.
- Share it with the world! — Once you’ve got something built, share it with the world! Tell people about what you’re building, why it’s awesome, and how they can follow you if they’re interested in knowing more about it.
