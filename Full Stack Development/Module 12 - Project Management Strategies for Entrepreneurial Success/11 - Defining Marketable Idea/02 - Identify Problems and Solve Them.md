# Identify Problems and Solve Them

- Good products and services solve a problem for someone.
- Identify pain points for people
  - What inconveniences them on a daily basis ?
  - How could you solve it ?
- If a product or service doesn’t solve a problem for anyone
  - It is useless → **discard it!**
- If you don’t know what to build
  - Talk to people.
  - Ask them what problems they have in their daily lives, work lives, personal products or tools or workflows.
  - Then identify how you might improve those with a product.
