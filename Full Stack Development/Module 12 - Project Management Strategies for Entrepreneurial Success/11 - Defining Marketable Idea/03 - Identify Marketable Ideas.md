# Identify Marketable Ideas

- Your time, energy, and hours in the day are *finite* — We want to weed out the bad or mediocre 99% of ideas as quickly as possible, so you can focus your energy and time on the truly excellent 1% ideas.
- Marketable ideas are those which could potentially return a profit.
- Non-marketable ideas are things that, while they might be a good idea, there is no way to sell them or get someone to pay you for it.
- Pick the ideas you think you could sell, and figure out how it would return a profit.
- If it’s realistic, and there’s an audience who might want to pay for it, it could be marketable idea.
