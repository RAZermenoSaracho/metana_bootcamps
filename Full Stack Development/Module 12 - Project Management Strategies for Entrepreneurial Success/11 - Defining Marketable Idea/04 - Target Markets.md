# Target Markets

**Target market**

- Who would you sell this to ? Who would view or consume or use it ?
- Do those people seem likely to pay for the product or service ?
- How much would they pay ?

**Ask people**

*Ask friends, family, peers, your social media audience*

- Would you be interested in this product or service if it existed ?
- Would you be willing to pay for it ?
- How much would it be worth to you ?
- What problem would it solve ?
- What other problems or pain points do you have, that no service or product out there is already solving ?
