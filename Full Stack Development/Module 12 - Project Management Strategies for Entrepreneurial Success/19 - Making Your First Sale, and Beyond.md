## After Your First Sale — What Next ?

- Your first sale is the starting point — Congratulations!— Now, you want to “rinse and repeat.”
- **Retain Your Customers**
  - Keeping customers is called “**customer retention**.”
  - A customer who’s bought from you once is more likely to buy from you again than a new potential customer who’s still deciding.
  - Customers stay due to quality service, quality products, loyalty to your brand, continuing engagement & communication, continuing feature releases and bonuses, and personal relationships.
  - You can build all of these into your business plan.
- **Techniques for Customer Retention**
  - **Quality Service**: Make sure they feel like they’re getting extra care.
  - **Loyalty to Your Brand**: Sell the story of why your brand is unique.
  - **Continuing Engagement & Communication**: Email, reach out, make phone calls, keep in touch with your customers.
  - **Continuing Feature Releases and Bonuses**: Software and products that continue to get new releases, updates, improvements feel more “alive” and fresh than something that’s built once and never updated again. Release updates periodically, and let your customers know about it.
  - **Build Personal Relationships**: Cultivate relationships with people who use your services and products. Introduce yourself. Reach out, make connections, ask questions. Friendships are mutually beneficial, and you can learn as much from them as they learn from you.
