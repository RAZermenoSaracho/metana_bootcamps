# Introduction to Databases

### **What is a Database ?**

A database is a structured collection of data. It’s like a digital filing system where information is organised and stored in a way that makes it easy to manage and retrieve.

### **What is a DBMS ?**

**DBMS**, which stands for **Database Management System**, is like a librarian for your data. Imagine you have a big collection of books. Instead of scattering them all over your house, you ask the librarian to organize them in a library. In this analogy, your collection of books represents your data. Your house represents your computer or storage system. The librarian represents the DBMS.
