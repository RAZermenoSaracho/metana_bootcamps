# What is Database hosts and ports

Imagine you have a friend living in a big city, and you want to send them a letter. In this scenario, the city is like the "database host," and your friend's apartment number is like the "port."

### What is the Database Host

This is essentially the address of the computer where your database lives. Just like a city has a specific location, a database host points to the specific computer (or server) on the internet or a local network where your database is stored. You need to know this address to connect to your database.

### What is a Port

Once you reach the right computer where your database is, you need to know how to get your information to the exact place it needs to go within that computer. Computers can run many different services (like email, web pages, and databases) at the same time, and each service listens for information coming in on a specific "port" number. Think of the port as the apartment number for your friend; it tells your information exactly where to go once it reaches the right computer.

So, when you want to connect to a database, you need to know both the host (where to go) and the port (how to get your information to the right place inside the computer).
