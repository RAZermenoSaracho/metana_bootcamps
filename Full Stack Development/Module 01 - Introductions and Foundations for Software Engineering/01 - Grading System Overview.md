# Grading System Overview

Welcome to the Metana Full Stack Bootcamp! This page provides an overview of how your performance will be evaluated throughout the course. Our grading system is designed to ensure that you receive comprehensive feedback on your understanding, practical skills, and overall progress.

## Course Components

- **Assignments – 70% of Final Grade**
  - Each of the 10 assignments contributes 7% toward your final grade.
  - Component Breakdown :
    - Completeness (*40%*) – All requirements and deliverables must be fully addressed.
    - Functionality (*30%*) – Your work must run smoothly and produce the expected outcomes.
    - Code Quality (*20%*) – Clean, well-organized, and maintainable code is key.
    - Timeliness (*10%*) – Meeting deadlines is crucial.
- **Agile Project – 30% of Final Grade**
  - This capstone project simulates a real-world full-stack application development scenario.
  - Component Breakdown :
    - Project Proposal & Planning
    - Front-End Development
    - Back-End Development
    - Database Integration
    - Deployment
    - Presentation & Demo

## Grading Scale

- **A (90-100%)** – Outstanding performance with deep understanding and excellent execution.
- **B (75-89%)** – Strong performance with minor areas for improvement.
- **C (60-74%)** – Meets expectations and shows potential for growth.
- **D (40-59%)** – Minimum requirements met; focus on improvement needed.
- **F (<40%)** – Below the requirements; an opportunity to reflect and improve.

## Extra Credit Opportunity

- Students can earn up to 5% extra credit by:
  - Adding unique, innovative features to projects
  - Demonstrating exceptional creativity in design and functionality
  - Exploring and integrating advanced technologies not covered in the course

This detailed system is here to guide you and ensure transparency in how your work is evaluated. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out to your course instructor.
