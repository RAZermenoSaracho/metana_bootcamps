# Introduction to Linux

Linux is a free-to-use operating system that's all about freedom and flexibility. Created by Linus Torvalds back in 1991, it's like the rebel of the operating system world. Unlike Windows or macOS, Linux is open-source, which means anyone can play around with its code, tweak it, or share it. It's like a giant, collaborative tech project ! ?

## How did it come about?

Unix is like the original car manufacturer that introduced the concept of automobiles to the world. They built the first cars and established certain design principles for vehicles. Over time, different companies adopted and adapted these principles to create their own cars, each with unique features but still following the fundamental concepts of driving on roads. ( Carl Benz invented the world's first car ).

Linux is a particular car model that emerged from the influence of Unix. It's like a reliable and versatile car that gained popularity among users. The Linux car follows the same road rules as Unix but offers additional features, customization options, and a collaborative community of mechanics (developers) who constantly improve and maintain it. ( Model T is regarded as the first mass-affordable automobile, which made car travel available to middle-class Americans. )

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/95/55/9555aeac55629d9839d9ce6df713a7eb0237c659af4beb569637acbe6295e860.png)

Linux distributions (distros) are different variants of the Linux car model. Each distro is like a customized version of the Linux car, with specific enhancements, features, and styles. Some distros are designed for speed and performance, others for user-friendly experiences, and some for specialized tasks like off-road adventures. Users can choose the distro that best suits their preferences and needs.

The kernel is the engine of the car, responsible for powering the entire vehicle and ensuring that it runs smoothly. Unix had its original engine design, and Linux built its engine inspired by the Unix principles. Distros are like cars that come equipped with the Linux engine, each offering its unique driving experience.
