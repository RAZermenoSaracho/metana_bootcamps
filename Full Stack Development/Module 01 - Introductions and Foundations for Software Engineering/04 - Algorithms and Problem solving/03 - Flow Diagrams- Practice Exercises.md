# Flow Diagrams: Practice Exercises

Please complete the following questions in Notion and submit your responses to your instructor for feedback.

1. Design a flowchart to calculate the average from 25 exam scores.
2. Flowchart for printing odd numbers less than a given number. It should also calculate their sum and count.
3. Flowchart to print the even numbers between 9 and 100.
4. Flowchart to find the greatest from 2 numbers.
