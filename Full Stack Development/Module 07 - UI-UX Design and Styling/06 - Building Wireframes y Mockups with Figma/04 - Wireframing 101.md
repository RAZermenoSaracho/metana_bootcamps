# Wireframing 101

- **Define Project Goals (Planning Stage)**
  - Identify the purpose of your website or app. What problem does it solve?
  - Define your target audience. Who are you designing for?
- **Choose Your Tools (Preparation Stage)**
  - Pen and paper (for a quick and low-tech approach)
  - Digital wireframing tool (for a more technical approach, many options available)
- **Sketch Main Page Layouts (Core Structure)**
  - Focus on the basic structure and content areas for key pages. Don't worry about aesthetics or details at this point.
- **Include Plans for Mobile/Responsive Versions** 
  - Use screenshots as a starting point.
  - Make sure each important view has a mobile-version counterpart.
- **Break Down Reusable Components (Efficiency)**
  - Identify elements that will appear on multiple pages (header, footer, navigation, etc.).
  - Create layouts for these reusable components.
- **Use Placeholder Images (Focus on Functionality)**
  - Use simple gray boxes or rectangles to represent where images will go. Don't spend time searching for perfect images now.
- **Sketch Important UI Elements (Interaction)**
  - Login screens
  - Navigation menus
  - Search bars
  - Popups and modal views
- **Illustrate List Content (Data Presentation)**
  - If your design involves lists (products, blog posts, etc.), sketch how this information will be displayed.
- **Refine and Iterate (Continuous Improvement)**
  - Get feedback from others on your wireframes.
  - Make adjustments to your initial sketches based on the feedback.

**Remember:** Wireframing is an iterative process. Keep it simple and focus on functionality over aesthetics in the beginning.
