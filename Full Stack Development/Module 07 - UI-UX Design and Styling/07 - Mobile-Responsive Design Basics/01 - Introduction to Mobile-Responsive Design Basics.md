# Introduction to Mobile/Responsive Design Basics

In today's digital landscape, ensuring that websites are accessible and functional across various devices is crucial. This is where mobile and responsive design basics come into play.

Mobile/responsive design is about creating websites that work well not only on desktop computers but also on mobile phones, tablets, and other devices with smaller screens. It's important to consider factors like readability, easy navigation, and image optimization to ensure a smooth user experience regardless of the device being used.

By focusing on mobile/responsive design basics, we aim to make websites usable and visually appealing across a wide range of devices, providing a seamless browsing experience for all users.

### Important Points

- **Readability:** Text should be easy to read.
- **Button and menu accessibility:** Buttons and menus should be easy to tap.
- **View orientation:** Design should adapt to different screen orientations.
- **Smaller image sizes:** Optimize images for faster loading on mobile.
