# Using Existing Designs as Guidelines for Wireframing

### Step 1 -> Find Your Template

Begin by identifying a website or template design that resonates with your project's goals and aesthetic preferences. Look for designs that align with the type of content and functionality you intend to include in your own project.

### Step 2 -> Download or Access Demo

Once you've found a suitable design, download the template or access its demo version if available. This allows you to examine the design closely and understand its layout, structure, and visual elements.

### Step 3 -> Capture Screenshots

Take screenshots of the pages and sections within the design that you want to emulate or draw inspiration from. Capture key components such as headers, footers, navigation menus, content sections, and interactive elements.

### Step 4 -> Follow Basic Page Layout

Use the screenshots as reference points to create wireframes for your own project. Follow the basic page layout and structure observed in the design, ensuring that you capture essential elements while adapting them to suit your specific requirements.

### Step 5 -> Consider Mobile and Responsive Views

Remember to capture screenshots of both desktop and mobile/responsive versions of the design. Pay attention to how the layout and elements adjust across different screen sizes. This ensures that your wireframes account for responsive design principles and provide a consistent user experience across devices.
