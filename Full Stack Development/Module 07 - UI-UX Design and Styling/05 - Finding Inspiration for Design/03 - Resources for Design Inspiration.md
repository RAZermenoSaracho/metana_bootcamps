# Resources for Design Inspiration

These are platforms and websites where designers can find ideas and examples to inspire their projects. They include community-driven platforms like Dribbble, general search engines like Google, and specialized template sites like Themeforest.

### Dribbble.com

#### **<https://dribbble.com/search/blog>**

- Dribbble is a leading platform for designers to showcase their work and find inspiration.
- Utilize the keyword search feature on Dribbble to explore a wide range of design concepts, including UI/UX designs, illustrations, and more.
- Dribbble is often regarded as a primary resource for design inspiration due to its extensive collection of high-quality design examples.

### Google Search

- Conducting a simple Google search for phrases like "great blog design examples" can yield valuable inspiration.
- Explore various search results, blog articles, and design showcases to discover innovative design ideas and trends.

### HTML / CSS Template Sites

- Platforms like Themeforest (<https://themeforest.net/search/blog>) offer a diverse selection of HTML and CSS templates for websites and web applications.
- Browse through Themeforest's collection to find ready-made templates that can serve as inspiration or be directly integrated into your project.
