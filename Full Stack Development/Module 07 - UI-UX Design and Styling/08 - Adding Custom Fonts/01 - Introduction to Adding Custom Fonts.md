# Introduction to Adding Custom Fonts

Adding custom fonts to your website allows you to personalize typography and enhance visual appeal. Different methods include using services like Google Fonts or hosting fonts locally. Each approach has its advantages, impacting ease of implementation, consistency across devices, and performance.

### Introduction to Using Google Fonts

Google Fonts (<https://fonts.google.com>) offers a vast collection of free, open-source fonts that you can easily integrate into your website. By utilizing Google Fonts, you can enhance the typography of your site, making it more visually appealing and engaging for your visitors. This approach allows you to choose from a diverse range of fonts, ensuring that you find the perfect style to match your website's aesthetic and branding.

### What is CDN Fonts ?

A Content Delivery Network (CDN) is a network of distributed servers that deliver web content to users based on their geographic location. When you use Google Fonts, the font files are hosted on Google's CDN servers, allowing them to be efficiently delivered to users worldwide. This helps to optimize the loading speed of your website by reducing latency and ensuring that visitors can access the fonts quickly and reliably regardless of their location.
