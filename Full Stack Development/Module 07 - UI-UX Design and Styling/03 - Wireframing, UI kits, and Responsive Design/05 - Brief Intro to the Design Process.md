# Brief Intro to the Design Process

Designing a website or app is like building a house. You need a plan, the right tools, and some creativity to make it happen.

### Step 1 -> Find inspiration for base design

Just like looking at pictures of houses to get ideas for your dream home, finding inspiration means checking out other websites or apps to see what looks good to you. It's like picking out your favorite house designs from a magazine.

### Step 2 -> Use simple wireframes to start, build mockups later

Think of wireframes as the blueprint or skeleton of your website or app. They're like drawing a rough sketch of your dream house layout before getting into the details. Mockups, on the other hand, are like adding colors and furniture to your blueprint to see how everything fits together.

### Step 3 -> Pick a UI framework or UI kit

UI frameworks or kits are like toolkits with ready-made parts for your website or app. They include things like buttons, menus, and forms that you can use to build your design faster, just like using pre-made Lego blocks to build a house instead of starting from scratch.

### Step 4 -> Build out design using your wireframes

Once you have your blueprint (wireframe), it's time to start building! You'll use the parts from your UI framework or kit to create each section of your website or app, like putting together puzzle pieces to make your dream house.

### Step 5 -> Add fonts and other polish

Finally, it's time to add the finishing touches. This means choosing the right fonts (like picking the right paint colors for your walls) and adding any extra decorations or details to make your design stand out, just like adding decorations and landscaping to your dream home.
