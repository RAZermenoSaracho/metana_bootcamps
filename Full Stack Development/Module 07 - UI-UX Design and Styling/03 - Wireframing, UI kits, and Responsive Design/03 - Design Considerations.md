# Design Considerations

When thinking about design, it's crucial to consider how your website will look and function. Focus on getting the most important things right, like buttons that work and easy navigation. Break down your website into smaller parts, like headers and buttons, so you can work on them one at a time. And remember, keeping things simple is better than making them complicated.

### How to think about your site design

This means considering what you want your website to achieve and who will be using it. For example, if it's a business website, you'll want it to be easy for customers to find information or buy products.

### Focus on making the essential parts work

Make sure the most important features of your website, like navigation menus or contact forms, are easy to use and understand. These are the things people will use the most, so they need to work well.

### What parts can you break out into components?

Instead of designing every single part of your website separately, think about which elements can be reused or repeated. This makes it easier to build and maintain your site, and it ensures consistency across different pages.

### Don’t make it complicated. Simple is best.

Keep your design clean and straightforward. Too many fancy features or complicated layouts can confuse users and make it harder for them to find what they need. It's better to focus on simplicity and clarity.
