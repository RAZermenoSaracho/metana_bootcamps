# Weekly Challenges 07 : Design Dynamo

Welcome to **Weekly Challenges 07: Design Dynamo!** This week’s challenges are crafted to spark your creativity and strengthen your skills in web design, wireframing, and styling using tools like Figma and TailwindCSS. Dive into these challenges to create beautiful, user-friendly designs and bring your ideas to life. Remember, you’re encouraged to **attempt at least one challenge within the week** to apply your skills in a fun, hands-on way. Let’s get designing!

---

### Challenge 1: **“UI Kit Creation Bonanza”**

**Description:**

Create a themed UI kit of your choice using Figma. Pick a fun and engaging theme, such as a favorite movie, Halloween, space exploration, or fantasy RPGs. Your UI kit should include buttons, input fields, navigation bars, icons, and other commonly used UI components. Make sure to design with responsiveness in mind.

- **Extensions:** Share your UI kit and receive feedback. Consider adding light and dark mode variations for extra creativity!
- **Skills Covered:** UI kit design, Figma, theme-based creativity, responsive design considerations.

---

### Challenge 2: **“Fictional App Design Challenge”**

**Description:**

Imagine and design a mobile app for a fun, fictional purpose using Figma. Examples could include “Unicorn Tracker,” “Alien Language Translator,” or “Superhero Training App.” Start with wireframes to outline the app’s core functionality and then create a polished, high-fidelity mockup with buttons, icons, and interactions that reflect your app’s unique theme and purpose.

- **Extensions:** Add interactive elements such as button clicks, page transitions, or pop-ups. Create a mini-presentation to showcase how users would interact with your app.
- **Skills Covered:** Wireframing, high-fidelity mockup creation, UI/UX design, interactivity in Figma.

---

### Challenge 3: **“UI Makeover Challenge”**

**Description:**

Choose an outdated or poorly designed webpage (you can find examples online) and give it a complete UI makeover using Figma. Redesign the layout with modern UI principles in mind, creating a fresh wireframe and then turning it into a polished, high-fidelity mockup. Focus on improving usability, visual appeal, and responsiveness.

- **Extensions:** Explain your design choices, such as changes made to color schemes, typography, and user flows, and share your prototype with peers for feedback.
- **Skills Covered:** Wireframing, Figma for UI/UX design, sourcing design inspiration, responsive and modern web design.
