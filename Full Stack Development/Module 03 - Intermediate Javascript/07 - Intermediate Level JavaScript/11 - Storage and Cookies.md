# Storage and Cookies

### What is a Cookie ?

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/be/18/be18dcc50b9efd72ffa747dfa27bc720128b4c13a18bd04c49b3a82b3fac59d1.png)

These are like tiny notes that a website can leave in your web browser. They are typically used for things like remembering your login status, language preferences, or what's in your shopping cart on an online store. Cookies are limited in size and can only store a small amount of information.

### What is setting a Cookie value ?

Setting a cookie value is like writing a note and leaving it in your browser. Websites use code to create and store these notes with information like your username or preferences.

### And now what is getting a Cookie value ?

When you return to a website, it checks if there are any notes (cookies) left in your browser. If it finds one with your username, for instance, it knows who you are without you having to log in again.
