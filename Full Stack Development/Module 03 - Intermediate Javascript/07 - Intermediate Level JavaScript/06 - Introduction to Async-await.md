# Introduction to Async/await

### What is Async/await ?

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/ce/db/cedba835ce7713e8a287fc2888b494775c8c57a6623ebe3482bd6fc18cb70234.png)

**Async/await** is like a way to do things step by step, even when some things take time. Imagine you're making a sandwich:

**Async Function (async)**: Think of this as the list of steps you're going to follow to make the sandwich. You declare your intentions, like "I'm going to make a sandwich," but you don't actually make it yet.

**Await Keyword (await)**: When you encounter something that takes time, like waiting for the toaster to toast your bread, you say "I'll wait for the toaster to finish before I move on." It's like you're patient and won't rush.

**Working with Promises**: Promises are like little notes you give to yourself. For example, you give a note to the toaster that says, "Let me know when you're done toasting." Then, you go do other things, like preparing the filling.

**Error Handling**: If something goes wrong, you have a plan for it. It's like having a backup sandwich in case the first one falls on the floor.

In simple terms, async/await helps you do multiple tasks, like making a sandwich, one after the other without getting impatient. You patiently wait for things to finish when needed and have a plan for when things don't go as expected, just like making a sandwich without rushing and being prepared for mishaps.
