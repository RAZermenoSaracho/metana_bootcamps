### What is a Dynamically Typed Language?

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/31/53/31530cf1df117862eb42261120bebc9cb874113e2dc2140a2b3afded7762c976.png)

Imagine you have a magic bag and you can fill it with books, and then the next day, it can hold toys, and the day after that, it can be full of snacks. You don't have to tell the backpack what you're going to put inside it beforehand; it just magically adjusts to whatever you put in it.

A dynamic typed language in programming, like JavaScript, works a lot like this bag. When you're writing a computer program, sometimes you need to store things, like numbers, words, or lists. In a dynamic typed language, you can change what you store without having to tell the computer in advance. So, you can start by storing a number in a place (like a variable), and later, you can change your mind and store a word there instead. The computer's programming language is smart enough to understand and adjust to these changes, just like the bag that can hold different things on different days.

This makes dynamic typed languages really flexible and fun to use, because they let you be creative and change your mind as you're writing your program!
