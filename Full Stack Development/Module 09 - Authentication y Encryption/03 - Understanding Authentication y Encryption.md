In this unit, we'll embark on a journey to explore the vital concepts of keeping digital information safe and secure. Before we dive into details, let's take a moment to understand why these topics are crucial and what you can expect to learn.

### Why Learn Authentication & Encryption ?

In today's digital age, where information travels across networks and resides on various devices, ensuring its security is paramount. Whether you're sending sensitive data over the internet, accessing confidential resources, or safeguarding personal information, understanding authentication and encryption is essential for protecting against unauthorized access and maintaining privacy.

### Why We Need Private Routes in Web Applications

Private routes are essential in web applications to restrict access to certain pages or functionalities based on user **authentication** and **authorization**. Without private routes, sensitive information or actions could be accessed by unauthorized users, leading to security breaches or misuse of resources. *Think of private routes like locked doors in a building. They prevent people from wandering into places they shouldn't be.*

### What is Authentication and Authorization

Authentication is like showing your ID to prove who you are. It makes sure you're really you before letting you in. Authorization decides what you're allowed to do once you're in. For example, just because you're in a building doesn't mean you can go into every room. You need permission (authorization) to access certain areas.

### Common Authentication Methods

When you sign in to a website with a username and password, that's **password-based authentication**. Another common method is using something called **JSON Web Tokens (JWT)**. These are like digital passes that prove you're allowed to access certain parts of a website.

### In This Module We Will Also Discuss About,

- **Conceptual Frameworks for Authorization:** Imagine different sets of rules for who can go where in a building. That's what frameworks like Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Discretionary Access Control (DAC) do for web apps.
- **Protection of Routes:** Just like how you lock doors in a building, we need to protect routes in web apps. This means making sure only the right people can access certain parts of a website.
- **Storage of Secrets on Client-side:** Sometimes, websites need to store secret information, like your login details. We have different ways to keep these secrets safe, like cookies or local storage on your computer.
- **Encrypting Secrets:** Encryption is like turning secret messages into secret codes. It keeps them safe from anyone who shouldn't see them. We use encryption to protect sensitive information like passwords or credit card numbers.
- **Best Practices for Security:** Just like how we lock our doors and keep valuables in a safe, there are smart ways to keep web apps safe too. Things like using secure connections (HTTPS), checking data carefully, and keeping everything up-to-date help keep websites secure.
