# Understanding Security for Cookies

- Cookies stick around until they expire, get deleted, or the user clears their browser data. This can be handy for keeping users logged in across sessions.
- Cookies provide seamless login experiences, allowing users to stay logged in without hassle.
- Storing sensitive data in cookies can expose it to potential threats. If someone gains access to the user's device, they might access this data.
- Cookies are designed to be private, accessible only by the website that created them.
- While cookies are generally safe, they're not foolproof. Malware or compromised devices can compromise cookie security.
- ***Avoid storing sensitive information like passwords directly** in cookies.* Encryption and other security measures should be used for sensitive data handling.
