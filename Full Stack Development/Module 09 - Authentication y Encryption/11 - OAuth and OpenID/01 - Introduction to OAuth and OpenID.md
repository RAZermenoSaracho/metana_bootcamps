# Introduction to OAuth and OpenID

OAuth (Open Authorization) and OpenID are protocols designed to facilitate secure access to web resources while verifying user identities through third-party providers such as Google, Facebook, Github, or other service providers.

### What are OAuth and OpenID?

In simple terms, OAuth and OpenID enable users to log in to web applications using their credentials from third-party providers. Instead of creating a new account for every website or application, users can use their existing accounts from trusted providers to authenticate themselves.
