# Introduction to Figma

### What is Figma ?

![](https://resources.metana.co/public/67/4f/674f5c78743d03056eae6fbaff5b997f7baf7429ffda6d08b2e5a6c11b487b2a.png)

Figma is like the cool kid in the design world. It's an online tool that lets people collaborate and create awesome designs together in real-time. Imagine Google Docs, but for designers. You can whip up anything from websites to apps, and everyone on your team can jump in and tweak stuff simultaneously.
