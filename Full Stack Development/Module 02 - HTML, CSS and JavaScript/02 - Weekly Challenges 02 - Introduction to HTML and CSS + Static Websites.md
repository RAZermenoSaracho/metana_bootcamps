### Pick Your Fun Task!

This week, the theme is creativity and interaction. Below are three fun and engaging challenges where you’ll create something cool with **HTML** and **CSS**.

The twist? You’re the one who decides how far you want to take it! Pick one of the challenges below, and don’t forget to make it your own by experimenting with animations, scroll effects, and even some interactive elements.

#### 1. **Fan Page for Your Favorite Game**

Build a single-page website dedicated to your favorite game. Think of this as a fan page where you introduce the game, show off some cool features, and share why it’s the best. Your page should be visually engaging, and you should use **images**, **text**, and **links** to make it informative and fun.

**Suggestions for Customization:**

- **Parallax scrolling**: Make different elements (like images) move at different speeds when the user scrolls.
- **Hover effects**: When users hover over game images or sections, have them zoom in or change color.
- **Transitions**: Use smooth transitions between sections for a more dynamic feel.

#### 2. **“Are You Worthy?” Best Friend or Life Partner Form**

Design a fun, quirky form where potential candidates have to answer questions to prove they’re worthy to be your best friend or life partner. This is your chance to get playful with the questions—think outside the box!

**Suggestions for Customization**:

- **Custom checkboxes and radio buttons**: Make them fun and unique to your form’s theme.
- **Input animations**: Add animations when users click into an input field (for example, the border could glow or the label could move).
- **Form validation**: Use CSS to give users feedback when they’ve missed a required field or entered something incorrectly.

#### 3. **Movie Tribute Webpage**

Create a tribute webpage for your favorite movie or TV show. This page can highlight key characters, plot summaries, and your favorite scenes. You could even include a “quote of the day” feature.

**Suggestions for Customization**:

- **Scroll-triggered animations**: Make elements fade in or slide up as the user scrolls down the page.
- **CSS Animations**: Make your favorite movie characters or quotes bounce, fade, or shake on the page.
- **Dynamic hover effects**: When users hover over a character’s picture, reveal a hidden fact or their best quote.

---

#### General Tips

- Use **Google Fonts** to find the perfect typography to match your theme.
- Play with **background gradients** or **patterns** to make your webpage stand out.
- Experiment with **CSS Grid** or **Flexbox** to create clean, responsive layouts that look good on any device.
- Add **smooth scroll** behavior so the page glides when navigating between sections.

Get creative, make it fun, and remember—you have the freedom to experiment and try new things. Feel free to reach out to your instructors if you get stuck or want feedback.
