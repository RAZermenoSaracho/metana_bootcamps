# Overview of Ethereum L2 Scaling solution

This video breaks down Ethereum Layer 2 scaling solutions, such as rollups, plasma, state channels, sidechains, and channels and explains how they help Ethereum overcome slow speeds and high fees by moving transactions off the main net (Layer 1) while still leveraging its security. It highlights different approaches, with a focus on how rollups efficiently bundle many transactions and anchor them back to Ethereum to scale performance securely
