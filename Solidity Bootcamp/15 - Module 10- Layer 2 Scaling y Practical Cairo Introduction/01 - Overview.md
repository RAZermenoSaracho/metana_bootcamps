# 🎯 Overview

## Module 10: Layer 2 Scaling & Practical Cairo Introduction

## Learning Objectives

- Learn the fundamentals of **Layer 2 scaling solutions** and their importance in improving blockchain performance.
- Gain a practical introduction to **Cairo**, the programming language for StarkNet.
- Understand the differences between **Optimistic Rollups** (e.g., Optimism, Arbitrum) and **ZK Rollups** (e.g., zkSync, StarkNet).
