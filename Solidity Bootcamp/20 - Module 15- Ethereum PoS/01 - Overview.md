## Module 15 – Ethereum PoS

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of Ethereum staking, including validator roles, effective balance, and rewards distribution.
- Learn about Distributed Validator Technology (DVT) and SSV infrastructure for improving validator security, decentralization, and resiliency.
