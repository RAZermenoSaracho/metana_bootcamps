# Ethereum Merge Update: End of PoW, Long Live PoS!

This video is about the Ethereum Merge, explaining the unification of the beacon chain and the execution chain, its significance for the Ethereum ecosystem, and outlining the agenda for the discussion on this major upgrade.

> *Note. : Be mindful of the fact that this video was created when ethereum was in Proof Of Work consensus. This video is let you understand difference between state of Ethereum pre and post Paris hard fork popularly known as “the merge”*
