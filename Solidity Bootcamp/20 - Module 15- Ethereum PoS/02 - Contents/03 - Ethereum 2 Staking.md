# Ethereum 2 Staking

This video is about Ethereum 2.0 staking, covering the shift from the original Casper contract to the beacon chain, the roadmap and timeline for Ethereum 2.0, the economics and requirements of being a validator, and the different options available for participating in proof-of-stake.

> *Note. Be mindful of the fact that this video was created pre paris fork, but it gives good over view on foundational concepts about staking on Ethereum which are true today as well accept the limit of ethereum staking amount has been increased to 2048 ETH instead of 32 ETH post pectra (Prague-electra) upgrade. Learn [here](https://ethereum.org/roadmap/pectra/) on more about Pectra upgrade*
