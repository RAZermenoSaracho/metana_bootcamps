# Slippage and Impermanent Loss

The video explains two major risks in decentralized exchanges (DEXs). For traders, the main risk is **price slippage**, where the executed trade price differs from the expected one due to low liquidity or large orders. For liquidity providers, the key risk is **impermanent loss**, which occurs when token prices diverge, causing their pooled assets to be worth less than simply holding them. Both risks highlight the trade-offs in AMM-based systems: traders must manage execution costs, while liquidity providers balance fee income against potential losses.
