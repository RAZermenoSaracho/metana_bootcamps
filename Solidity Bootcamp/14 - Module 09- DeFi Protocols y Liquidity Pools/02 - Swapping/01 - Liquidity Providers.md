# Liquidity Providers

This video explains that in DeFi, anyone can become a liquidity provider (LP) by adding tokens to pools on platforms like Uniswap or Balancer and earn fees from trades. It explains that LP tokens (ERC-20) represent your share of the pool. It also explains that you can withdraw your assets anytime by burning these LP tokens.
