# Constant Product AMM

This video explains a smart contract for a constant product AMM, covering state variables and the constructor, internal functions like mint and burn, and public functions for swap, addLiquidity, and removeLiquidity. It also includes a demo showing contract deployment and performing liquidity additions, swaps, and removals.
