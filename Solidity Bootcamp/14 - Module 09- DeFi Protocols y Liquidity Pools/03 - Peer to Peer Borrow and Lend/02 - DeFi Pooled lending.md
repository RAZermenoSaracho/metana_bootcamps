# DeFi Pooled lending

This explains pooled lending in DeFi, where users deposit funds into a pool that others can borrow from, similar to a bank. It also covers concepts like collateral, cryptocurrency volatility, and flash loans, highlighting how these mechanisms work in decentralized finance.
