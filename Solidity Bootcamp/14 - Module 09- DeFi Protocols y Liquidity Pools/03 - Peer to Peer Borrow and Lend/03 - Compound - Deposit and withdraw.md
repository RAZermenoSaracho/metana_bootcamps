# Compound – Deposit and withdraw

This video explains how to lend and borrow tokens on Compound Finance. It focuses on the lending operations – **supply** to earn interest and **redeem** to withdraw tokens. The process involves transferring your ERC-20 token to a contract, approving the corresponding cToken, and calling the mint function to supply your tokens and receive cTokens in return.
