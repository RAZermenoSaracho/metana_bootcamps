# Aave – Flash Loan

This video explains flash loans—uncollateralized loans that must be borrowed and repaid within the same transaction—commonly used for arbitrage, liquidation, and DeFi hacks. It also shows how to write and test a Solidity smart contract for a flash loan on Aave.
