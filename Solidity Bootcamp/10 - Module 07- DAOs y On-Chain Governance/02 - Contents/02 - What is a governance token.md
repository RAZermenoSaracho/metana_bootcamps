# What is a governance token?

Provides a concise overview of governance tokens in the blockchain ecosystem. It explains that governance tokens are cryptocurrencies granting holders voting rights on decisions like protocol upgrades, fee structures, and treasury management, thereby promoting decentralized, community-driven governance in projects such as DAOs and DeFi platforms.
