# What is Chainlink?

This video explains Chainlink as a decentralized oracle network that connects real-world data to smart contracts, solving blockchain’s limitation of being unable to access off-chain information. It shows how oracles provide verified data, earn rewards in LINK tokens, and risk losing them if dishonest. The video also covers Chainlink’s architecture, tokenomics, and its role in making smart contracts truly useful in real-world applications.
