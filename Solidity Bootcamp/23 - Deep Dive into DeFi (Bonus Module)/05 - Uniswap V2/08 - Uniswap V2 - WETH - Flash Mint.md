# Uniswap V2 – WETH – Flash Mint

Explains **flash minting**, a method that lets you create tokens out of thin air within a single transaction, use them (e.g., for arbitrage), and burn them by the end. Unlike flash loans that borrow existing tokens, flash minting creates temporary supply. The video demonstrates how this can be used for arbitrage even when no actual liquidity exists and shows a Solidity example using **WETH10** to mint more tokens than the total supply, use them, and repay—all in one atomic transaction.
