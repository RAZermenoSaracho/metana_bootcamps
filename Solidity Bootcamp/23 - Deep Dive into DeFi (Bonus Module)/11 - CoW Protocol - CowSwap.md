# CoW Protocol – CowSwap

This video explains **Cow Protocol**, the trading mechanism behind **CowSwap**, which adds a thin batching layer on top of existing AMMs like Uniswap. This batching allows multiple trades to be aggregated into a single Ethereum transaction, enabling peer-to-peer matches between users with opposite trade needs. As a result, it reduces transaction costs, price impact, and LP fees, offering better prices. Additionally, CowSwap acts as a **meta-DEX aggregator**, querying multiple platforms to ensure users always get the best trade execution.
