# 🎯 Overview

## Deep Dive into DeFi (Bonus Module)

This is an **optional module** designed for learners who wish to gain a **deeper understanding** of advanced DeFi mechanisms.

It covers topics such as:

- How **Automated Market Makers (AMMs)** like **Uniswap** and **Balancer** operate to enable decentralized trading.
- The concepts of **staking**, **yield farming**, and **liquidity provisioning**, and how these mechanisms **incentivize participation** in DeFi protocols.
