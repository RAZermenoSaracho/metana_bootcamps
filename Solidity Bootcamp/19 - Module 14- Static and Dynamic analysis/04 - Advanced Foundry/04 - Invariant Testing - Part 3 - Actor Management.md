# Invariant Testing – Part 3 – Actor Management

Further advances invariant testing by adding the concept of actors in Foundry’s invariant tests. Actors represent different entities (e.g., multiple users or contracts) interacting with the system. The video likely shows how to manage multiple actors in your handler or test, assigning them different roles, and ensuring the invariant testing framework uses these different actors to perform actions. This helps simulate more realistic scenarios (like multiple users interacting) while checking that key invariants (like no theft of funds, etc.) hold true.
