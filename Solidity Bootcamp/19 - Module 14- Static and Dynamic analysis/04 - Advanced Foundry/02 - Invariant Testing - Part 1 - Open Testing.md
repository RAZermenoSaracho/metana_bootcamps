# Invariant Testing – Part 1 – Open Testing

Begins a series on invariant testing with Foundry. Part 1 likely covers the basics of invariant tests—explaining what invariants are (conditions that should always remain true, no matter what sequence of actions is taken) and setting up a simple invariant without complex state machine (open testing might mean without constraints). It might show an example invariant (e.g., token total supply doesn’t change) and how to have Foundry continually generate random sequences of calls to try to break that invariant.
