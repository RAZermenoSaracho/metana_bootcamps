# EIP 1559 Explained

This section explains **EIP-1559** introduced after the London hard fork. Earlier, all gas fees went to miners, but now part of the fee is **burned** and part goes to miners/validators. It clarifies the different fee terms – **base fee, max fee, and priority fee** – and how they appear differently across platforms like **Etherscan, Blocknative, and MetaMask**. It also highlights that the **max base fee must be ≥ the current network base fee** for transactions to be accepted, and that fees are generally shown in **gwei**.
