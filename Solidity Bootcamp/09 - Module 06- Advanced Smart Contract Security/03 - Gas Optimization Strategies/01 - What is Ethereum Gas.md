# What is Ethereum Gas

This video provides an in-depth explanation of gas in the Ethereum network. It describes gas as a unit used to measure the computational effort required for transactions and smart contract executions. The video also delves into how gas prices fluctuate based on network demand and how users can optimize their transactions to minimize fees.
