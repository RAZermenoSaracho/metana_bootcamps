# Prevent ReEntrancy Attacks

Watch from **01:36:15 to 02:03:31**, the video walks through a **step-by-step guide to reentrancy attacks**, beginning with the classic Vault example and then progressing into more advanced cases like cross-function, cross-contract, and read-only reentrancy. It shows how each type exploits external calls made before internal state updates, with increasing nuance and complexity. Finally, it explains a practical method to systematically identify and assess potential reentrancy vectors and their maximum impact.
