# Chainlink Oracle

The video explains what oracles are, why blockchains can’t access external data directly, and how this creates the “oracle problem.” It highlights the risks of centralized oracles and introduces Chainlink as a decentralized solution that securely connects smart contracts with real-world data. Viewers are encouraged to explore Chainlink further to build reliable, data-driven blockchain applications.
