# What is MEV?

This video explains **Maximal Extractable Value (MEV)**, the hidden game where validators, traders, and bots reorder blockchain transactions to maximize profits, often at users’ expense. It covers how MEV works through the mempool, its origins with miners, and the distinction between harmful and healthy uses.
