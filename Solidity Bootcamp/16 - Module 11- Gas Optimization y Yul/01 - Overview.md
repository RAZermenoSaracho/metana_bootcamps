# 🎯 Overview

## Module11 – GasOptimization&OptionalYulModule

## Learning Objectives

- Understand how EVM opcodes and bytecode-level optimizations can improve smart contract performance and reduce gas costs.
- Explore MEV protection techniques such as Flashbots and private mempools, with an optional introduction to Yul and inline assembly for advanced optimization.
